My Top 3 Dream Destinations

Tokyo, Japan

Country: Japan

I want to visit Tokyo to walk through the neon streets of Akihabara and Shibuya, try real Japanese ramen and sushi, and see how one of the world's biggest cities mixes ancient temples with crazy technology.

Rome, Italy

Country: Italy

I want to visit Rome to stand inside the Colosseum, throw a coin in the Trevi Fountain, and taste fresh pasta and gelato in the city where so much of world history began.

Sydney, Australia

Country: Australia

I want to visit Sydney to see the Opera House up close, swim at Bondi Beach, and watch the harbour from a ferry on a sunny day.
