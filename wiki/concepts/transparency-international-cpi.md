# Transparency International Corruption Perceptions Index (CPI)

An annual ranking of countries by perceived levels of public sector corruption, scored 0 (highly corrupt) to 100 (very clean). Published by Transparency International. Cambodia's score has declined every year since its peak: 24 (2022) → 22 (2023) → 21 (2024) → 20 (2025). In 2025, Cambodia ranked 163rd out of 182 countries — second-to-last in ASEAN, ahead only of Myanmar. The global average is 42. For comparison: Singapore scores 84 (3rd globally), Malaysia 52, Vietnam 41. Cambodia's declining trajectory indicates worsening corruption despite government rhetoric about reform.
