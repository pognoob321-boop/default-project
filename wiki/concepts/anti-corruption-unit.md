# Anti-Corruption Unit (ACU)

Cambodia's central institution for investigating corruption, established in 2010 under the Anti-Corruption Law. The ACU has gained some public and private sector support through MOUs, but lacks genuine independence and is widely perceived as ineffective. It receives complaints (520 in 2024, including 141 anonymous) but has not produced meaningful prosecutions of high-level officials. The 2010 law provides no whistleblower protection — whistleblowers can be jailed up to 6 months if they report corruption that cannot be proven. International observers (U4 Anti-Corruption Centre) note "the political will to fully eradicate corruption has been missing."
