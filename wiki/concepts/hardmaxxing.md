# Hardmaxxing

> Semi-permanent or permanent changes to appearance through medical procedures or surgery. Higher cost, higher risk, faster visible results.

## Definition

Hardmaxxing refers to invasive interventions that physically alter facial or body structure. It includes cosmetic surgery, medical treatments, and procedures that permanently or semi-permanently change appearance.

## Key Characteristics

- **Invasive**: Requires medical procedures or surgery
- **Often permanent**: Results are long-lasting or irreversible
- **Higher cost**: Significant financial investment required
- **Higher risk**: Potential for complications, dissatisfaction, or need for revision
- **Faster visible results**: Changes are more dramatic and immediate

## Common Hardmaxxing Procedures

### Facial Surgery
- Rhinoplasty ($5K-$15K) — Nose reshaping, permanent
- Jaw surgery/BSSO ($5K-$40K) — Skeletal advancement, permanent
- Chin implant/genioplasty ($3K-$15K) — Chin projection, permanent
- Jaw implants ($5K-$40K) — Jawline definition, permanent
- Blepharoplasty ($2K-$5K) — Eyelid surgery, permanent

### Non-Surgical Medical
- Botox ($300-$800) — Muscle relaxation/slimming, 3-6 months
- Dermal fillers ($500-$4K) — Volume addition, 6-18 months
- PDO thread lift ($300-$2K) — Semi-permanent lifting, 12-18 months
- Chemical peels ($100-$800) — Skin resurfacing
- RF microneedling ($500-$3K) — Skin tightening, scarring

### Orthodontics
- Invisalign/braces ($3K-$8K) — Teeth alignment, permanent
- Palatal expansion — Widens upper jaw

### Hair Restoration
- FUE/DHI hair transplant ($1.5K-$15K) — Permanent
- PRP therapy ($200-$800) — Hair density
- Finasteride + minoxidil ($30-$200/month) — Hair loss prevention

### Skin Treatments
- Isotretinoin/Accutane ($100-$300/month) — Severe acne, permanent results
- CO2 laser ($500-$3K) — Skin resurfacing
- Chemical peels — Skin texture improvement

## Decision Framework

### When to Consider Hardmaxxing
1. After fully committing to softmaxxing for 6-12 months
2. When specific structural issues remain that significantly impact appearance
3. When you can afford it without financial stress
4. After consulting multiple qualified professionals
5. When expectations are realistic

### When NOT to Hardmaxxing
- While still overweight, with bad skin, or poor grooming
- When expecting surgery to "fix everything"
- When chasing perfection (one procedure leads to another)
- When motivated by comparison to others rather than personal goals
- When mental health concerns (body dysmorphia) are present

## The Golden Rule

**Stack both**: A solid softmaxxing foundation with targeted hardmaxxing for specific features that genuinely bother you. Not the other way around.
