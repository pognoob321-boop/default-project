# Patronage System

A governance model where political power is maintained through personal loyalty networks rather than institutional processes. In Cambodia, the CPP under Hun Sen built a patronage system since the 1990s where tycoons received logging, mining, and land concessions in exchange for loyalty and political funding. By 2012, five tycoon-senators held 20% of total allocated land (500,000+ hectares). This system is the root cause of Cambodia's systemic corruption — it makes institutions subordinate to political loyalty, enables organized crime, and prevents genuine reform. As analyst Seng Vanly stated: "The core problem is not academic credentials but political will. Even leaders with advanced degrees will struggle to curb corruption if they do not reform what is a patronage-based system."
