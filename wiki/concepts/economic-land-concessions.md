# Economic Land Concessions (ELCs)

Long-term leases (originally 70–99 years, reduced to 50 years in 2015) granting state land to private companies for industrial agriculture. Created in the 1990s–2000s when the post-Khmer Rouge government lacked resources for land management. The 2001 Land Law caps ELCs at 10,000 hectares, but numerous concessions far exceed this limit. Over 2.2 million hectares have been granted, directly accounting for up to 40% of Cambodia's total deforestation. ELCs are the primary mechanism through which Cambodia's political elite and foreign investors seize land from communities, displacing an estimated 777,000 people. Despite covering 2.2M+ hectares, the government expected only $4M in tax revenue from ELCs in 2025.
