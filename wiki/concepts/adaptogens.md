# Adaptogens

Plant-derived compounds that modulate the body's stress response, primarily through HPA axis regulation and cortisol reduction. Key nootropic adaptogens include Bacopa monnieri (best-evidenced memory herb; requires 8–12 weeks for effects), Rhodiola rosea (reduces mental fatigue under stress but does not enhance baseline performance), Ashwagandha (anxiolytic, cortisol-lowering), and Panax ginseng (working memory in some trials). Unlike stimulants, adaptogens generally do not produce acute cognitive effects — benefits emerge with consistent use over weeks. They are generally well-tolerated but have contraindications (e.g., Rhodiola with manic-depressive psychosis, Ginseng with hypertension).
