# Cognitive Enhancement

The improvement of cognitive functions (attention, memory, executive function, processing speed) beyond normal baseline through pharmacological, behavioral, or technological means. In the nootropics context, this spans a spectrum from restorative use (treating deficits in cognitive decline, ADHD, or post-COVID impairment) to enhancement use (boosting normal performance in healthy individuals). Public attitudes are more accepting of restorative use. Ethical debates center on fairness (access inequality), coercion (normalized pressure to enhance), authenticity (are achievements "less one's own?"), and the therapy-vs-enhancement distinction. Non-pharmacological approaches (exercise, sleep, cognitive training) remain the most evidence-backed enhancement strategies.
