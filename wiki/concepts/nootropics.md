# Nootropics

Substances — synthetic, natural, or dietary — that purportedly improve cognitive functions such as attention, memory, learning, motivation, and executive function. The term was coined in 1972 by Corneliu Giurgea, who established strict criteria: must enhance memory, protect the brain, and have extremely low toxicity. Modern usage has expanded well beyond these original criteria. Effects are generally modest; no supplement reliably produces supranormal cognition in healthy individuals. Non-pharmacological interventions (exercise, sleep, nutrition) remain the strongest "nootropics."
