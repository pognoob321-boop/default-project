# Looksmaxxing

> Online self-improvement practice focused on maximizing physical attractiveness through systematic appearance optimization.

## Definition

Looksmaxxing is the systematic practice of improving one's physical appearance through lifestyle changes, grooming, fitness, style, and sometimes medical or surgical interventions. The term combines "looks" with "maxing" (maximizing).

## Etymology

- Originated on incel message boards in the 2010s
- Popularized on TikTok in the 2020s
- Shortlisted for Collins Dictionary's 2024 Word of the Year
- Over 16 billion TikTok views
- Part of the broader "-maxxing" suffix trend from gaming/RPG culture

## Subcategories

### Softmaxxing
Non-invasive, reversible improvements: skincare, grooming, fitness, style, posture, sleep, nutrition. Where 90% of results come from.

### Hardmaxxing
Semi-permanent or permanent medical/surgical interventions: rhinoplasty, jaw surgery, fillers, Botox, hair transplants, orthodontics. Higher cost, higher risk.

### Appealmaxxing
Maximizing overall attractiveness appeal including aura, presence, body language, charisma, and lifestyle signals.

## Key Principles

1. **Skincare is highest ROI** — Clear skin alone transforms appearance
2. **Body fat reduction reveals bone structure** — Most people think they have weak jawline when it's obscured by fat
3. **Sleep, hydration, and nutrition** — Foundation of skin and overall appearance
4. **Softmaxxing before hardmaxxing** — Maximize low-risk methods first
5. **Static photos ≠ real life** — Expression, movement, voice, and confidence matter in person

## Risks

- Body dysmorphia and obsessive comparison
- Anxiety and shame from constant self-scrutiny
- Physical harm from DIY procedures
- Financial risk from expensive procedures
- Mental health deterioration

## Medical Consensus

- Healthy looksmaxxing (grooming, fitness, sleep) is beneficial
- Bonesmashing is dangerous and unscientific
- Mewing has limited evidence for adults; may help developing children
- Surgery should be last resort after softmaxxing optimization
- Professional medical advice essential for hardmaxxing decisions
