# Racetams

A class of synthetic nootropic compounds built on a pyrrolidone backbone, originally developed starting with piracetam in 1964. They primarily modulate AMPA glutamate receptors (enhancing long-term potentiation) and cholinergic systems (increasing acetylcholine turnover). The family includes piracetam, aniracetam, oxiracetam, pramiracetam, phenylpiracetam, coluracetam, and Noopept. Potency varies widely — Noopept is approximately 1000x more potent than piracetam. Choline co-supplementation (Alpha-GPC, citicoline) is commonly recommended to prevent headaches from choline depletion. Most racetams are prescription-only in the UK and EU; piracetam is not FDA-approved in the US.
