# Cholinergic System

The neurotransmitter system centered on acetylcholine (ACh), critical for memory encoding, attention, and learning. ACh is synthesized from choline and acetyl-CoA, and its signaling is modulated through synthesis, release, receptor activation, and enzymatic breakdown (via acetylcholinesterase). Nootropics target this system at multiple points: choline precursors (Alpha-GPC, citicoline) provide raw material for synthesis; acetylcholinesterase inhibitors (huperzine A, donepezil) prevent breakdown; and racetams increase ACh turnover. This is why choline supplementation is commonly paired with racetams — increased turnover depletes available choline, causing headaches.
