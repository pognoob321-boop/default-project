# Nootropics — Comprehensive Research

## Definition & History

The term "nootropic" was coined in 1972 by **Corneliu E. Giurgea**, derived from Greek _nóos_ (mind) + _tropḗ_ (turning). He synthesized **piracetam** in 1964 — the world's first nootropic — and established five criteria for the category:

1. Enhance learning and memory
2. Protect learned behaviors against disruption (hypoxia, shock)
3. Protect the brain against physical/chemical injury
4. Increase efficacy of tonic cortical/subcortical control
5. Exhibit extremely low toxicity and minimal side effects

Modern usage has expanded well beyond Giurgea's strict definition to include any substance — synthetic, natural, or dietary — that purportedly improves cognition. There is no globally accepted clinical definition.

## Classification

### Racetams (Pyrrolidone Nootropics)
Synthetic compounds built on a pyrrolidone backbone, modulating AMPA/NMDA glutamate receptors and cholinergic systems.

| Compound | Potency (vs piracetam) | Key Properties |
|---|---|---|
| Piracetam | Baseline | General cognitive support |
| Aniracetam | 3–5x | Creativity, mood, anxiolytic |
| Oxiracetam | Mild | Learning, memory, mildly stimulating |
| Pramiracetam | Potent | Focus without stimulation |
| Phenylpiracetam | 30–60x | Focus, energy, physical performance (banned in sports) |
| Coluracetam | Potent | Memory, visual processing |
| Noopept | ~1000x | Peptide analog; upregulates BDNF/NGF |

### Cholinergics (Acetylcholine Enhancers)
- Alpha-GPC, Citicoline (CDP-Choline) — choline sources that cross BBB
- Huperzine A — acetylcholinesterase inhibitor from _Huperzia serrata_
- Donepezil, Rivastigmine, Galantamine — prescription AChE inhibitors for Alzheimer's

### Stimulants / Eugeroics
- Caffeine — adenosine receptor antagonist; most consumed psychoactive
- Modafinil/Armodafinil — wakefulness-promoting; prescription
- Methylphenidate (Ritalin) — dopamine/norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor
- Theacrine, Methylliberine — caffeine-like but tolerance-resistant

### Adaptogens & Botanicals
- Bacopa monnieri — best human RCT evidence of any memory herb
- Lion's Mane (_Hericium erinaceus_) — stimulates NGF synthesis
- Rhodiola rosea — reduces mental fatigue under stress
- Ashwagandha — anxiolytic, cortisol-lowering
- Ginkgo biloba — cerebral circulation; mixed evidence in healthy young adults

### Amino Acids & Precursors
- L-Theanine — promotes alpha waves; calm focus; synergistic with caffeine
- L-Tyrosine — dopamine/norepinephrine precursor; stress performance

### Nutraceuticals
- Creatine monohydrate — brain energy buffer (ATP/phosphocreatine)
- Omega-3 (DHA/EPA) — structural membrane support
- B-Vitamins — metabolic cofactors for neurotransmitter synthesis

## Mechanisms of Action

1. **Cholinergic modulation** — acetylcholine is critical for memory encoding and attention
2. **Glutamatergic (AMPA) modulation** — racetams enhance long-term potentiation (LTP), the cellular basis of memory
3. **Dopaminergic stimulation** — stimulants increase DA/NE in prefrontal cortex, enhancing attention and motivation
4. **Cerebral metabolism & blood flow** — enhanced glucose/oxygen utilization, vasodilation, improved erythrocyte plasticity
5. **Neuroprotection** — antioxidant, membrane-stabilizing, antihypoxic effects
6. **Neurotrophic support** — Lion's Mane stimulates NGF; Noopept upregulates BDNF/NGF in hippocampus
7. **Stress axis modulation** — adaptogens reduce cortisol via HPA axis

## Evidence Tiers

### Tier 1 — Strong Human Evidence

| Substance | Finding | Dose |
|---|---|---|
| Caffeine | Most studied; reliably improves alertness, reaction time, attention | 100–200 mg |
| L-Theanine | 2026 meta-analysis (31 RCTs, n=1168): SMD=0.51 for attention; potential antidepressant (SMD=0.69) | 100–200 mg |
| Caffeine + L-Theanine | Synergistic calm focus; most-studied combination | 100:200 mg |
| Creatine | 2024 meta-analysis (16 RCTs, n=492): memory SMD=0.31; especially beneficial under sleep deprivation | 3–5 g/day |
| Modafinil | Strong evidence for wakefulness, executive function, accuracy in complex tasks | 100–200 mg |

### Tier 2 — Moderate Evidence

| Substance | Finding | Duration Needed |
|---|---|---|
| Bacopa monnieri | Memory consolidation, learning rate; best-evidenced memory herb | 8–12 weeks |
| Rhodiola rosea | Reduces mental fatigue under stress; no baseline enhancement | Days–weeks |
| Lion's Mane | Pilot RCT: improved cognition in older adults with MCI; stimulates NGF | 8–16 weeks |
| Citicoline/Alpha-GPC | Attention enhancement; choline + cytidine delivery | Weeks–months |
| Omega-3 (DHA) | Structural support; benefits in deficient populations | Months |

### Tier 3 — Insufficient / Overhyped

- Piracetam in healthy adults — no demonstrated benefit
- Ginkgo in adults under 60 — no convincing evidence
- Many herbal nootropics acutely — require weeks of use; single doses don't work
- Microdosing psychedelics — extremely limited clinical data

**Medical consensus:** Exercise, sleep, and nutrition produce stronger cognitive benefits than any supplement. Exercise robustly increases BDNF and improves memory via hippocampal neurogenesis.

## Risks & Safety

### By Category
- **Stimulants:** Insomnia, anxiety, elevated HR/BP, dependence risk, psychosis at high doses
- **Racetams:** Headache (choline depletion), insomnia, GI discomfort
- **Herbals:** Bacopa = GI/thyroid; Ginkgo = blood thinning; Ashwagandha = sedation

### Serious Concerns
- Long-term effects unknown in healthy individuals
- Possible paradoxical cognitive decline with chronic use
- **48% of surveyed supplement products contained undeclared prescription drugs** (2025 EU/Australia study, Vanhee et al.)
- No nootropics safe during pregnancy/breastfeeding

## Legal Status

| Region | Key Rules |
|---|---|
| US | Supplements legal (DSHEA); piracetam not FDA-approved; Rx stimulants need prescription |
| UK | Herbal legal as food supplements; racetams are prescription-only |
| EU | Novel Food Regulation; piracetam Rx in most countries |
| Russia | Most lenient; piracetam, Noopept, phenibut widely accessible |
| Australia | Piracetam = Schedule IV; modafinil = Rx only |
| Canada | Supplements legal; racetams cannot be commercially sold |

## Common Stacks

### Foundation Stack (Most Studied)
- Caffeine 100–200 mg + L-Theanine 100–200 mg — synergistic calm focus

### Brain Energy Stack
- Creatine 3–5 g/day + Omega-3 (DHA) 1–2 g/day

### Memory Stack
- Bacopa 300 mg (evening) + Alpha-GPC/Citicoline 300–500 mg (morning) + Lion's Mane 500–1500 mg

### Stress-Performance Stack
- Rhodiola 200 mg (morning) + Ashwagandha 300–600 mg (evening) + L-Theanine 100–200 mg

## Ethical Considerations

- ~10% of UK university students use modafinil non-prescribed
- Majority do NOT view it as cheating (similar to caffeine)
- Concerns: fairness, coercion ("neurodoping"), authenticity
- Most universities classify as drug misuse, not academic integrity violation
- Military use sanctioned by Singapore, US, India, France air forces

## Key Takeaways

1. Effects are real but modest — no "Limitless pill" exists
2. Exercise, sleep, and nutrition outperform any supplement
3. Safety varies enormously; supplement market is poorly regulated
4. Research advancing in exogenous ketones, multi-ingredient formulations, personalized nootropics
5. Most evidence is for acute alertness (caffeine, modafinil) and long-term memory (bacopa, creatine)
