# Cambodia Expensive Schools

> Comprehensive research on premium and elite international schools in Cambodia (2025-2026 data).

## Market Overview

- Phnom Penh concentrates the majority of international schools (50+ claiming "international" status, 500+ with international vocation across all categories)
- Cambodia ranked among Asia's cheapest cities for international schools in 2022-2023
- 2023 median price: USD $5,120/year; maximum fees recorded: >$20,000/year
- Fee tiers: Affordable ($1,500-6,750), Mid-range ($6,750-19,750), Premium ($19,750-31,500)
- Annual fee increases: typically 3-8% per year

## Top-Tier Premium Schools (USD $19,000-31,500/year)

### 1. International School of Phnom Penh (ISPP)
- **Founded:** 1989 (oldest international school in Cambodia)
- **Curriculum:** Full IB continuum (PYP, MYP, DP)
- **Fees (2025-26):**
  - PreK: $9,011/year
  - Elementary (KG-Grade 5): $19,749-$23,300/year
  - Middle School (Grade 6-10): $23,811-$27,544/year
  - High School/IB Diploma (Grade 11-12): $25,714-$29,533/year
  - Early Years 1 (age 3): $9,558; Early Years 2 (age 4): $11,680
- **One-time fees:** $250 application + $3,500 entrance (KG-12)
- **Annual capital fee:** $2,782
- **Characteristics:** Non-profit, 50+ nationalities, ~1:9 teacher-student ratio, 90%+ IB diploma pass rate, Olympic-size pool, professional theater, campus with science labs
- **Sibling discounts:** 5% off tuition for 3rd child, 10% from 4th
- **Source:** ISPP School Fees page; US Dept of State 2025 Fact Sheet

### 2. Northbridge International School Cambodia (NISC)
- **Founded:** 1997
- **Curriculum:** Full IB continuum (PYP, MYP, DP); member of Nord Anglia Education network
- **Fees (2025-26):**
  - Kindergarten: $21,460/year
  - Grade 3: $21,752/year
  - Secondary grades higher; IB Diploma highest
- **One-time fees:** $290 application + $850 entrance + $1,000 refundable deposit + $1,500 KG transition fee
- **Annual capital fee:** $2,990
- **Bus:** $2,080/year per child
- **EAL:** Tier 2: $1,250/semester; Tier 3: $2,195/semester (up to $4,390/year)
- **Sibling discounts:** 5% for 3rd child, 10% from 4th
- **Characteristics:** 8-hectare campus, Juilliard performing arts partnership, MIT STEAM programs
- **Year-1 cost (2 children, KG + Grade 3):** ~$60,132 total

### 3. Australian International School Phnom Penh (AISPP)
- **Curriculum:** Australian national curriculum + IB (PYP, MYP, DP, CP)
- **Fees:** Tuition ranges ~$5,000-$26,000/year (grade dependent); high school IB Diploma years exceed $25,000
- **One-time fees:** $250 application
- **Sibling discounts:** 5% for 2nd child, 10% for 3rd, 15% from 4th
- **Characteristics:** 6-hectare campus in Russey Keo, 600-seat theater, gymnasium, football field, swimming pool, butterfly garden, WASC & CIS accredited

### 4. Shrewsbury International School Phnom Penh
- **Curriculum:** National Curriculum for England (IGCSE, A-Levels)
- **Fees:** Premium band ($12,000-$25,000/year depending on grade)

- **Characteristics:** Nearly 5 centuries of British heritage, new 7.2-hectare Sen Sok campus opening September 2026

### 5. Canadian International School of Phnom Penh (CIS)
- **Curriculum:** Alberta program + IB Diploma + bilingual (English, French, Mandarin)
- **Fees (2025-26, in KHR):**
  - Nursery 2 (age 2): KHR 26,867,001 (~$6,700)
  - KG 1 (age 3): KHR 38,897,001 (~$9,700)
  - KG 2 (age 4): KHR 48,120,001 (~$12,000)

## What Premium Tuition Covers

**Included at top-tier schools:**
- Purpose-built campuses with Olympic pools, science labs, theaters, sports complexes
- Western-trained, internationally licensed teachers (often native English speakers)
- Small class sizes (18-22 students)
- Extracurricular activities, sports leagues (PPAC8, MRISA conferences)
- University counseling (starting Grade 9)
- IB diploma pass rates above global average

**Typically NOT included (billed separately):**
- Registration/application fee: $30-$300
- Capital/infrastructure fee: $300-$2,990/year
- Bus transport: ~$2,080-$4,500/year per child
- School lunch: $400-$700/year
- Uniform: $150-$350 first year
- EAL support: $1,250-$4,390/year
- Exam fees (IGCSE, A-Level, IB Diploma)
- Textbooks and learning materials

## Key Considerations for Parents

1. **Check accreditation:** CIS, WASC, IB World School status are quality indicators
2. **Ask about EAL costs** before enrolling non-native English speakers
3. **Budget 3-5% annual fee increases**
4. **Review withdrawal notice requirements** (typically 60 days written notice)
5. **Compare sibling discount structures** across schools
6. **Consider curriculum alignment** with future relocation plans (IB is most portable)
7. **Visit campuses** and request language assessments before commitment
