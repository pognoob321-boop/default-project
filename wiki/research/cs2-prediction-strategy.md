# CS2 (Counter-Strike 2) — Predicting Match Winners

## Summary

CS2 prediction relies on map pool analysis, economy management, opening duel win rates, and utility usage rather than flashy stats like K/D. The map veto process is the single highest-leverage moment — teams have dramatically different win rates on individual maps, and predicting the veto sequence gives a structural edge most bettors ignore.

## The #1 Edge: Map Pool Analysis

1. Find each team's permaban (map they always ban first)
2. Find map overlap (maps both teams are comfortable on)
3. Check map-specific win rates (75% vs 40% on a map = massive edge)
4. Predict the veto based on historical bans/picks
5. Bet map winner, not just match winner

## Stats That Actually Predict Wins

| Stat | What It Means | Why It Matters |
|------|---------------|----------------|
| Opening Duel Win Rate | First kill of the round | First kill shifts win probability from 50% to 70-75% |
| Trade Kill Ratio | How fast team responds to teammate's death | Shows discipline and coordination |
| Economy Management | Winning rounds while damaging opponent's economy | Teams that manage economy win more long games |
| Utility ADR | Damage from grenades/flashes | More predictive than raw K/D |
| CT/T Side Win Rates | Performance on each half separately | Some maps are CT-sided (Nuke, Overpass) |

## Stats That DON'T Predict Wins

- Overall tournament K/D (noisy, inflated by farming weak teams)
- AWP Rating (snipers have inflated stats by nature)
- Map win rate without opponent strength context

## Pre-Match Checklist

1. Confirm rosters — Missing IGL or AWPer = structural disadvantage
2. Last 10 matches only — CS2 meta evolves fast, ignore >3 months
3. Map pool matchup — Which maps likely, who's stronger on each
4. H2H filtered by context — Only count LAN results if it's a LAN match
5. Patch impact — Recent weapon/map changes flip team strengths
6. Tournament context — Elimination vs already-qualified = different intensity

## Live Betting Windows

| Moment | Why | Edge |
|--------|-----|------|
| After pistol round | Winner gets 2-round rifle advantage | Books take ~45 seconds to price — bet fast |
| Halftime on Nuke/Inferno | Side-switch reshapes odds | Team down 4-8 on T side of Nuke may be favored |
| Team hits 15 rounds | Close-out rate at match point ~80% | Live odds often only price 65-70% |
