# Looksmaxxing Wiki — Full Verification Pass

Audit of all 32 looksmaxxing-related pages in this wiki (23,452 words) against
peer-reviewed literature and clinical guidance. Method: claim extraction from
every page, then targeted verification via NCBI E-utilities, PMC full text,
PubMed systematic reviews, and position statements from AAO / AAD.

## Headline

**Of ~90 checkable claims: roughly 15 survive as stated. About 20 are partly
right. Over 50 are unsupported, misstated, or actively wrong.**

The failure is not random. It follows four repeatable patterns:

1. **Real paper, mangled number.** "Bruxism = 300x bone apposition" and
   "BMI 2.38 = attractiveness threshold" are both real papers with the wrong
   figure, the wrong sex, or the wrong label attached.
2. **Community consensus promoted to science.** The entire ratio table in
   `facial-ratios.md` traces verbatim to looksmax.org threads. No paper
   derived it. The wiki never says so.
3. **Norm confused with ideal.** Population average ≠ attractiveness optimum.
   The nasolabial angle shows a ~15-degree gap between the two.
4. **Mechanistically plausible, never trialed.** Most of the debloating list,
   castor oil, topical eyebrow finasteride.

## Two claims are actively harmful

| Claim | Where | Why harmful |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| Hydrocortisone on the nose to slim it | `nose-under-eye.md:1-21` | No benefit, plausible temporary vasoconstriction only. Chronic topical steroid on thin nasal skin → cutaneous atrophy, telangiectasia, steroid rosacea. Classic trigger for topical steroid-induced rosacea. **Delete.** |
| "Water loading then abruptly cutting water" for debloating | `lip-improvement.md:95` | Reale 2018 (PMID 29182412): bloating **peaked during the loading phase** (main effect p=0.0005) and extra mass loss was 0.6%. The protocol causes the puffiness it claims to remove. **Delete.** |

Three more carry real risk without the "delete" verdict:
- **Brimonidine (Lumify)** rebound hyperemia — the wiki presents it as a free win.
- **Zinc 30-40 mg** is exactly the NIH tolerable upper limit (40 mg/day), with
  copper-depletion risk. Not a target dose.
- **Topical finasteride on eyebrows** is systemically absorbed with no validated
  safety margin for off-face use; teratogenic residue risk.

## Top 10 errors to fix first

1. **"Bruxism = 300x mandibular angle bone apposition"** — no source anywhere.
   Real: Turp 2021 found ~50% of mandibular angles apposed in adult bruxism
   patients; the control group was **adolescents 12-18**, not adult
   non-bruxers, so no bruxism-vs-nonbruxism odds ratio can be computed.
   Hayek 2022 found 52% apposition with **no control group at all**.
   Popescu 2025 gives ~3x bone apposition and 15x masseter hypertrophy.
   300x appears in none of them.
2. **"Mandibular angle should be close to 90°"** (`looksmaxxing-deep-dive.md:130`)
   — norm is ~124±6° in adult males. Off by ~35°.
3. **"Re & Rule 2016: BMI decrease of 2.38 kg/m² is the perceptual threshold for
   attractiveness improvement"** — verified directly against the paper.
   2.38 is **women only**; **men are 2.59**. The 1.33 figure is the
   weight-perception threshold, not an attractiveness threshold. The real title
   is "Heavy Matters," *Soc Psychol Pers Sci* 7(1):69-76.
4. **"SPF 30+ prevents 90% of visible aging"** vs **"UV causes 80%"** — the wiki
   contains both, contradicting itself. The 80% figure traces to Flament 2013
   (DOI 10.2147/CCID.S44686). The 90% figure has **no source**. The
   defensible RCT number is Hughes 2013, *Ann Intern Med*: daily sunscreen gave
   **24% less skin aging** over 4.5 years (relative odds 0.76, 95% CI 0.59-0.98;
   PMID 23732711).
5. **"Bizygomatic:bigonial ≈ 1.35"** — arithmetically inconsistent with the
   wiki's own figure. If bigonial is 85.5-92% of bizygomatic, the inverse is
   1.09-1.17, not 1.35. Real CT data lands at ~1.30 (Sertel Meyvaci 2021:
   137.6mm / 106.0mm).
6. **"Hard diet = longer rami, soft diet = shorter rami"** — **direction is
   backwards.** Katz 2017 (PNAS, PMC5576786) found historical farmers had a
   *narrower* ramus; Inoue 2019 (*Sci Rep*) found hard-diet mice had a
   *shorter* ramus.
7. **"Tongue pressure 5-10 g vs 500-1000 g needed for skeletal change"** —
   invented. Measured tongue pressure is ~52 kPa (Arakawa 2021, PMID 32799377;
   anterior tongue 56.0 kPa, Wu 2020). Optimal tooth-movement force is 28-44 g
   for tipping, 110-137 g for translation (Li 2021, PMID 34183652). Bite force
   is 500-700 **newtons**, not grams.
8. **"Body fat 12-15% = maximum facial definition"** — no peer-reviewed source
   sets a BF% threshold for facial definition. Literature uses BMI or facial
   morphometrics. Pure gym folklore.
9. **"Skin quality beats masculinity as an attractor"** — contradicted by
   Foo, Simmons & Rhodes 2017 (*Sci Rep* 7:39731): masculinity predicted
   attractiveness in men; skin condition did not.
10. **"Castor oil grows eyebrows"** — no RCT exists. Zero controlled human
    evidence.

## What actually survives

These held up and are safe to keep:

- **Reducing body fat improves facial attractiveness.** Direction is real
  (Windhager 2013; de Jager 2018 meta-analysis, r=0.71 perceived adiposity vs
  BMI). Only the specific percentages are invented.
- **Averageness predicts attractiveness.** d=0.52 across 18 papers (Rhodes 2006).
- **Symmetry predicts attractiveness, weakly.** r=0.30 rated, 0.19 measured
  (Rhodes 2006); Weeden & Sabini 2005 found little effect in men specifically.
  Much of it disappears when perceived health is controlled — it is largely a
  health cue.
- **Eye separation ≈46% of face width** (Pallett, Link & Lee 2010, PMID 19896961).
  This one forum number happens to be independently correct — coincidence, not
  derivation.
- **Mouth breathing associates with longer, narrower faces** (Grippaudo 2016,
  n=3017). Correlation established; causality not.
- **Sunscreen works, and 24% is the honest number.**
- **Tretinoin is the reference anti-aging retinoid.** AAD backs it.
- **Adapalene is genuinely OTC in the US for 0.1% gel since 2016.** Cream and
  0.3% gel remain prescription. UK/EU: prescription (POM), OTC only via the
  adapalene+BPO combination. Cambodian status: unverified — do not assert.
- **Oral collagen peptides do work** — the "stomach breaks it down" objection
  is a category error; hydrolyzed peptides survive digestion. Meta-analysis of
  26 RCTs, n=1721 (Pu 2023, PMID 37432180). Effect real but modest, and much
  of the literature is industry-funded. The wiki calls it a waste; that is wrong.
- **Bimatoprost lash timing is correct** — 8 weeks visible, 16 weeks plateau
  (FDA label).
- **Bonesmashing: the wiki is right and the medical consensus is right.**
  Keep this section.

## The structural fix

`facial-ratios.md` needs a standing header saying the table is **looksmax.org
community consensus, not research**, plus a norm-vs-ideal column. Without that,
every reader treats forum numbers as clinical targets. The single most useful
edit in this whole pass is that one line.
