# Teen Energy + Brain Protocol — Cambodia

Created: 2026-09-27. Deliverable written for user. Prices are Phnom Penh 2025–26 field estimates (psar + Lucky), ±20%; verify locally. 4,000 riel = $1.

## Evidence anchors
- Sleep debt → attention/learning: **strong** (adolescent sleep restriction studies, AAP start-school Later).
- Morning daylight → circadian phase + alertness: **strong**.
- Active recall + spaced practice → retention: **strong** (Roediger/Karpicke, Dunlosky).
- Resistance training + adequate protein in adolescence: **strong** for strength/body comp, growth-neutral.
- Creatine 3–5 g/d: **strong** adult strength/cognitive-under-sleep-deprivation; **moderate/limited** teen-specific data, no harm signal at that dose (ISSN/ISSP).
- Fish oil for cognition in healthy teens: **weak** (effect ≈ null); cardiovascular: moderate.
- Multivitamin if diet already adequate: **weak**.
- Magnesium for sleep: **moderate** only if intake deficient (common on rice-heavy diets).
- Iron: **only if deficient** — self-dosing unsafe (flag).
- Energy drinks in adolescents: **strong harm signal** (sleep disruption, palpitations, dependency).
- Fasting adolescents: **unsafe/flagged** — excluded.

## Cambodia-specific dangers ranked
1. Late-night phone + 6:30 school start → chronic sleep debt.
2. Energy drinks (Club X, BC, Coca-Cola Energy) + skipped breakfast → caffeine/sugar rollercoaster.
3. Heat sweat + plain water only / sugary iced drinks → dehydration + electrolyte drop.
