# Organized Crime, Drug Trafficking, and Corruption Networks in Cambodia

## Summary

Cambodia has become a hub for transnational organized crime spanning cyber scams, drug trafficking, money laundering, wildlife trafficking, and arms smuggling. Chinese criminal organizations dominate the landscape, operating through casino complexes and special economic zones with the protection of Cambodia's political elite. The Huione Group laundered at least $4 billion and processed $49 billion in crypto transactions. Cambodia's 174 licensed casinos serve as fronts for money laundering and scam operations. The U.S. has imposed escalating sanctions on senators, tycoons, banks, and corporations. A 2025 study concluded: "Cambodian state institutions systematically and insidiously support and protect the criminal networks involved in transnational fraud."

## Drug Trafficking

- Record 9.5 tons of crystal meth seized in 2024 (Cambodia's largest ever)
- 200 tons of toxic precursor chemicals removed from Phnom Penh labs (2022)
- 10 Chinese nationals sentenced to life for trafficking 14 tons of chemicals
- Meth treatment seekers tripled in four years to 9,200+ (2024)
- Cambodia sits astride key trafficking routes from Myanmar's Golden Triangle

## Casino Industry

- 174 licensed casinos by 2023 (up from 101 in 2021)
- Casinos repurposed into scam compounds after COVID-19 collapsed junket model
- Amnesty found 12 casinos directly linked to scam compounds with torture and forced labor
- Casino junket operators were "the cornerstone of a massive underground banking system"

## Chinese Organized Crime

- Chinese criminal networks are the primary architects and operators of Cambodia's scam industry
- Estimated 500,000 scammers work across Southeast Asia, many in Chinese-run compounds
- U.S. Select Committee (May 2026): "Once-fragmented enterprises have converged into transnational networks built for scale, speed, and profit"
- Chinese state-owned enterprises built much of Sihanoukville's infrastructure — later repurposed as scam compounds
- By 2019, 150 of 156 hotels in Sihanoukville were Chinese-owned

## Wan Kuok-koi ("Broken Tooth") — 14K Triad

- Boss of Macau chapter of 14K Triad (20,000 members)
- Imprisoned 1999–2012 for murder, bombings, arms smuggling
- Post-prison: Built empire across Cambodia, Myanmar, Palau
- Member of CCP's Chinese People's Political Consultative Conference
- Sanctioned by U.S. Treasury (Dec 2020)
- Founded World Hongmen Association as triad front
- Stated: "I used to fight for the cartels, and now I fight for the CCP"
- Still free and active as of December 2024

## Money Laundering

### Huione Group
- Processed $49 billion in crypto (2021–2026) — largest online criminal marketplace in history
- Hun To (PM's cousin) held 30% ownership of Huione Pay
- FinCEN designated as "primary money laundering concern" (May 2025)
- License revoked by Cambodian National Bank (Dec 2025)
- At least 6 Cambodian banks sanctioned, shut down, or liquidated by U.S.

### Casino-Based Laundering
- Casinos function as "a shadow banking system" moving money jurisdiction-to-jurisdiction with almost no restriction (UNODC)
- Methods: cash-in/cash-out, junket financing, crypto conversion, underground banking

## Wildlife Trafficking

- Operation SANDOKAN (2026): 5 major trafficking kingpins — all Chinese nationals
- Kill at least one tiger every week
- Tiger bone: $1,463–$1,975/kg; Rhino horn: $19–$26/gram
- Scam compound bosses were largest domestic consumers until late 2025
- Traffickers routinely bribe port and customs officials
- 3 tons of pangolin scales seized in Cambodia-bound container (Feb 2026)

## Arms Trafficking

- Cambodia is a transit country for small arms into Myanmar
- Chinese organized crime groups in Sihanoukville use small arms
- Military officers charged with arms smuggling
- U.S. arms embargo imposed citing corruption and Chinese military influence
- Chinese weapons now make up ~90% of Cambodia's arsenal

## The "Mafia State" Assessment

A 2025 study by Jacob Sims concluded: "Cambodian state institutions systematically and insidiously support and protect the criminal networks involved in transnational fraud and related human trafficking... The evidence suggests that CPP elite are now substantially abetted by a sophisticated international criminal professional class. Yet, the state-party explicitly permits them to operate as economic partners due to the massive flows of patrimonial resources captured from their activities."
