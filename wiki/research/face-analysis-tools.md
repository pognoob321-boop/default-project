# Face Analysis & Rating Tools — Deep Research

> Researched: 2026-07-27

## Overview

AI-powered face rating and facial analysis tools have exploded in popularity, driven by TikTok virality and the looksmaxxing community. These tools analyze facial structure, bone architecture, symmetry, and proportions to generate scores and improvement recommendations.

- **70+ precise facial ratios** calculated per analysis

## Competitors

### PSLScore (pslscore.com)

**Focus:** PSL-scale based facial aesthetics analysis

- **8 feature categories:** eye area, jawline, midface, nose, symmetry, skin quality, harmony, sexual dimorphism
- **15+ quantitative measurements:** canthal tilt, gonial angle, midface ratio, facial W/H ratio, interpupillary distance
- Personalized looksmaxxing recommendations based on specific weak categories
- Free tier available; iOS + Android apps
- Transparent methodology

### Umax

**Focus:** Most well-known face rating app, broad mainstream appeal

- Polished UX, large user base, strong brand recognition
- Improvement suggestions and looksmaxxing content ecosystem
- **Weakness:** Scoring methodology less transparent; subscription pricing criticized
- Best for casual users who want a quick score

### Qoves

**Focus:** Premium, research-backed facial analysis

- Excellent educational content (YouTube, blog)

## Common Features Across Tools

### What Gets Measured

- **Bone structure:** Jawline, cheekbones, brow bone, chin projection, mandible angle
- **Proportions:** Facial thirds, fifths, golden ratio, midface ratio, facial W/H ratio
- **Eye area:** Canthal tilt, eye spacing, eyelid exposure, interpupillary distance
- **Nose:** Bridge, tip, width, projection, nasofrontal angle
- **Skin:** Clarity, texture, oiliness, dark circles, acne
- **Symmetry:** Left-right facial balance, asymmetry index
- **Sexual dimorphism:** Masculine/feminine trait balance
- **Hair:** Hairline depth, density, eyebrow shape

### Scoring Frameworks Used

- **PSL Scale** (Prettyscale/Sluthate/Looksmax) — 1-10 compressed rating
- **Golden Ratio** (Phi = 1.618) — Proportional ideal
- **Frankfort Plane** — Standardized head position for measurement
- **Ethnicity/Gender/Age Cohort Benchmarking** — Context-specific ideals

### Improvement Recommendations

- **Softmaxxing:** Skincare routines, hairstyle, grooming, posture, mewing, facial exercises
- **Hardmaxxing:** Surgical options (implants, bimax, CCW rotation, rhinoplasty)
- **Lifestyle:** Nutrition, sleep, hydration, debloating strategies

---

## Key Insights

1. **Scores are NOT comparable across platforms** — Different methodologies, scales, and weights
2. **Measurement-based apps produce more consistent results** than black-box scoring
3. **Free tiers are limited** across all apps — detailed analysis always behind paywall
4. **Privacy varies widely** — Always check photo handling policies before uploading
5. **No AI rating captures full attractiveness** — Missing: voice, body language, motion, personality, grooming context
6. **FaceIQ Labs is the most feature-rich** but also among the most expensive; criticized for score inflation
7. **Symme is best for guided improvement protocols** backed by peer-reviewed research
8. **Qoves is best for academic rigor** but premium priced
9. **For casual use:** Umax or RateMyLooks.ai for quick scores
10. **For bone structure specifically:** FastShot AI or Aura Looksmaxxing
