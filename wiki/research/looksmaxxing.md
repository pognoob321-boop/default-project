## 1. What Is Looksmaxxing?

**Looksmaxxing** (LOOKS-mak-sing) is the systematic practice of maximizing one's physical attractiveness through lifestyle changes, grooming, fitness, style, and sometimes medical or surgical interventions.

- **Origin**: Coined on incel message boards in the 2010s; popularized on TikTok in the 2020s by male content creators
- **Cultural context**: Part of the "manosphere" vocabulary; associated with the "black pill" ideology (the belief that female sexual selection is primarily based on external physical qualities)
- **Mainstream adoption**: Shortlisted for Collins Dictionary's 2024 Word of the Year; over 16 billion TikTok views
- **Academic recognition**: Published peer-reviewed papers in *Indian J Otolaryngol Head Neck Surg* (2026), *Facial Plastic Surgery & Aesthetic Medicine* (2025), and others

**Key distinction**: Looksmaxxing is the umbrella term. Under it sit multiple subcategories (softmaxxing, hardmaxxing, appealmaxxing, etc.).

>
> **1. The BMI figure was wrong three ways.** Verified directly against Re &
> Rule 2016, "Heavy Matters," *Soc Psychol Pers Sci* 7(1):69-76:
> **2.38 kg/m² is for women; men are 2.59 kg/m².** And **1.33 kg/m²** is a

> Skin apparent *health* does predict attractiveness (Jones et al. 2004,

**Skincare:**

**Body Composition:**
- Resistance training changes facial appearance by lowering body fat

- Lean muscle mass + low body fat reveals jawline and bone structure

**Grooming:**
- Eyebrow grooming (clean up strays without over-shaping)

- Haircut matched to face shape

**Lifestyle:**

5. **Stack**: Best results come from solid softmaxxing foundation + targeted hardmaxxing

## 5. Appealmaxxing — Maximizing Overall Appeal

**Definition**: A holistic approach to maximizing overall attractiveness beyond just physical features — encompassing aura, presence, body language, charisma, and lifestyle signals.

### Appealmaxxing Components

**Facial Appeal:**
- Hair styling (can provide 1-tier boost in perceived attractiveness)
- Proper grooming (eyebrows, beard, stubble)
- Glasses (can hide poor eye area or facial asymmetry)
- Piercings/earrings (can add uniqueness)
- Skincare (foundation of facial appeal)

**Body Appeal:**
- Physique development
- Posture optimization
- Style/fashion choices
- Movement patterns

**Non-Physical Appeal:**
- Body language and confidence
- Voice quality
- Social skills and charisma
- Lifestyle signals (statusmaxxing)
- Eye contact and presence

### Appeal vs. Raw Bone Structure

Community debate exists about what constitutes "appeal":
- **Robust/strong bone structure** (high sexual dimorphism) = "mogger" appearance
- **Soft/pretty features** (lower dimorphism, refined features) = "pretty boy" appeal
- Research suggests both can be attractive; what matters is facial harmony and how features work together
- Static photos ≠ real life; expression, movement, voice, and confidence all affect perceived attractiveness

## 6. Bone Structure & Facial Aesthetics

### What Determines Facial Bone Structure

**Genetic factors (mostly fixed):**
- Craniofacial bone development
- Sexual dimorphism level (masculine/feminine feature balance)
- Eye area anatomy (orbital rim, canthal tilt, eyelid exposure)
- Jawline structure (ramus length, gonial angle)
- Cheekbone projection
- Nose structure

**Potentially modifiable factors:**
- Body fat percentage (reveals existing bone structure)
- Masseter muscle development (chewing)
- Tongue posture during development (mewing — limited effect in adults)
- Medical/surgical intervention

### Key Facial Aesthetics Metrics

**Jawline:**
- Gonial angle (angle of the jaw)
- Ramus length (vertical portion of jaw)
- Jaw width relative to face
- Mandibular plane angle

**Eye Area:**
- Canthal tilt (positive = outer corner higher)
- Eyelid exposure (hooded vs. visible crease)
- Orbital rim prominence
- Interpupillary distance

**Cheekbones:**
- Projection (how far forward)
- Width relative to face
- Zygomatic arch definition

**Nose:**
- Bridge straightness
- Tip projection and rotation
- Width proportionate to facial frame
- Nasolabial angle

**Overall:**
- Facial symmetry
- Facial thirds (hairline to brow, brow to nose base, nose base to chin)
- Facial width-to-height ratio
- Sexual dimorphism level

### The Science of Bone Remodeling

**Established science:**
- Bone adapts to mechanical load over time (same mechanism behind orthodontics and weightlifting-induced bone density)
- This is well-established and not controversial

**Mewing (orthotropics):**
- Theory: Correct tongue posture creates upward/forward pressure on palate during development
- For children/teens: Reasonable evidence that oral posture influences facial development
- For adults: Meaningful structural bone change without medical intervention is **unlikely**
- American Association of Orthodontists does not recommend mewing
- Journal of Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery (2019): Insufficient evidence for bone change from tongue posture alone in adults
- Won't hurt, but don't expect results in adults

**Bonesmashing:**
- Literally hitting face with blunt objects to trigger bone remodeling
- **Dangerous and ineffective**: Can cause permanent bone deformity, nerve damage, chronic pain, dental problems
- No respected doctor endorses it
- Published concerns in *J Stomatol Oral Maxillofac Surg* (2024)

### Problem-Solution Matrix by Budget

**Weak/Recessive Chin:**
- Free: Beard lengthening, posture correction, photography angles
- Low ($50-500): Dermaroller + mewing protocol, jaw exercises
- Mid ($500-3K): Chin filler 1-2ml (HA, 6-18 months)
- High ($3K-15K): Sliding genioplasty or chin implant (permanent)

**Weak Jawline/Poor Definition:**
- Free: Body fat reduction to 10-12%, mewing, chewing, beard shaping
- Low: Jaw exercises, masseter chewing protocol
- Mid ($1K-4K): Jawline filler 2-4ml, masseter botox for slimming
- High ($5K-40K): Jaw implants, BSSO orthognathic surgery

**Flat/Underprojected Cheekbones:**
- Free: Body fat reduction, contouring technique, lighting
- Mid: Cheek filler 1-2ml per side
- High: Malar implants, fat grafting

## 7. Mewing & Orthotropics — Deep Dive

**What it is:** Practice of maintaining correct tongue posture — entire tongue pressed against palate, lips sealed, nose breathing.

**Correct technique:**
1. Tongue placement: Entire tongue against roof of mouth; tip behind front teeth without touching
2. Suction hold: Light suction, not forceful pushing
3. Teeth position: Lightly touching or slightly apart; no clenching
4. Lips: Closed, no tension
5. Breathing: Always through nose

**Evidence summary:**
- **Supported for development**: Children/teens with developing bones show effects from oral posture
- **Supported for orofacial function**: 2025 systematic reviews found genuine evidence for improved nasal breathing, swallowing mechanics, tongue-thrust correction
- **Supported for sleep apnea**: 2021 meta-analysis found myofunctional therapy significantly reduced apnea-hypopnea index
- **NOT supported for adult bone change**: No credible evidence mewing reshapes jawline/facial structure past early childhood

## 8. Psychological & Health Considerations

### Risks
- **Body dysmorphia**: Constant self-scrutiny can lead to distorted body image
- **Anxiety and shame**: Feeling pressured to "fix" parts of oneself
- **Obsessive comparison**: Comparing to digitally optimized strangers
- **Physical harm**: DIY procedures, extreme dieting, bonesmashing
- **Financial risk**: Expensive procedures with uncertain outcomes

### Healthy vs. Toxic Looksmaxxing
- **Healthy**: Grooming, fitness, sleep, dressing well — benefits anyone
- **Toxic**: Self-hatred, obsessive comparison, irreversible procedures to copy someone else's face
- **Key question**: Are you building yourself up (healthy) or tearing yourself down (toxic)?

### Mental Health Flags
- Preoccupation with specific facial features
- Inability to find contentment after improvements
- Constant comparison to others
- Feeling that appearance equals worth
- Anxiety about being judged

## 9. What Actually Works — Evidence-Based Priority

### Highest-ROI Changes (Ranked)

1. **Skincare routine** (cleanser + active serum + moisturizer + SPF) — cheapest, fastest, highest-ROI
2. **Body fat reduction** — reveals jawline and bone structure; no supplement compares
3. **Resistance training** — changes body composition, posture, and facial appearance
4. **Sleep optimization** — 7-9 hours consistently
5. **Haircut optimization** — right cut for face shape
6. **Teeth whitening/straightening** — bright smile, high impact
7. **Posture improvement** — immediate visual and confidence boost
8. **Hydration** — 2-3L daily
9. **Wardrobe basics** — well-fitting clothes
10. **Fragrance** — primal level of attraction

### What Doesn't Work (or Has Minimal Evidence)
- Mewing for adults (bone change)
- Bonesmashing (dangerous, no evidence)
- DIY orthodontics
- Extreme/unregulated supplements
- Jawline chewing devices (risk of TMJ damage)
