# Facial Analysis Guides (looksmax.org)

Catalog of high-effort facial-analysis / facial-ratio threads found while
ingesting the user-provided Definitive Facial Analysis Guide.

## Summary

Seven threads converge on the same core ratio set with tightened ranges.
The 2026 "Definitive" guide is the current state of the art: it keeps the
4-pillar scoring model (HARM/MISC/ANGU/DIMO) from the Nov 2025 original but
fixes a penalty bug that could reward *lower* harmony scores.

## Takeaways

- Use thread #1 for scoring a face into a single number; use #4 for measuring individual ratios correctly.
- The 2026 penalty fix matters: old spread penalty could make a 9 HARM score *worse* than a 7 HARM score with everything else equal.
- Point-weight systems (#3) reveal community priority: gonial angle, eye separation, canthal tilt, facial thirds, FWHR, cheekbone setness dominate.
- Threads themselves flag limits: ratios ≠ full attractiveness (phenotype, coloring, soft tissue still matter); many ideals are derived from model samples, not controlled studies.
