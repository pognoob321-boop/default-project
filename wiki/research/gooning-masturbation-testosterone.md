# Gooning / Masturbation / Testosterone — What Research Actually Says

Fact-check of nofap vs gooning claims. Sources verified against PubMed/journals, not forum folklore.

## Verdict table

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Masturbation lowers testosterone long-term | **FALSE** | No proven long-term effect (Cleveland Clinic; Healthline review of literature) |
| Abstinence raises T by 45% permanently | **FALSE** | 45% figure = Jiang 2002 (n=28): *temporary* day-7 peak that returns to baseline even with continued abstinence |
| Masturbation acutely changes T | **TRUE, minor** | 2021 RCT pilot (Basic & Clinical Andrology, n=11): may slightly slow circadian free-T drop; total T:T:C ratios unchanged; T returns to baseline ~10 min after orgasm (2020 study) |
| Poor sleep lowers T | **TRUE, strong** | Leproult & Van Cauter, JAMA 2011: 1 week @ 5h/night → daytime T **down 10–15%** in young healthy men |
| Deep sleep drives growth hormone | **TRUE, strong** | Van Cauter & Plat, J Pediatr 1996: ~70% of GH pulses in men coincide with slow-wave sleep; largest pulse = first hours of night |
| Porn causes ED in all young men | **CONTESTED** | 2026 systematic review (11 studies 2013–2023): mere frequency NOT a significant predictor; *problematic use* correlates more. Jacobs 2021 (n=3419): problematic porn score ↑ → ED probability ↑; masturbation frequency not significant |
| Porn addiction = drug addiction | **OVERSTATED** | Voon 2014 fMRI (PLOS ONE, 19 vs 19): CSB group showed stronger ventral striatum/dACC/amygdala response to explicit cues ("wanting ≠ liking" split like addiction) — but small n, correlation not causation; Voon herself cautions against calling it addiction |
| "Porn addict" self-label = disorder | **NO** | ICD-11 CSBD (Kraus 2018, World Psychiatry): impulse-control disorder, NOT substance/behavioral addiction grouping. Explicitly: do NOT diagnose common adolescent masturbation or distress from moral judgments alone. Requires 6+ months of failed control + functional impairment |
| Weekly short sessions = harmful | **FALSE** | No evidence of harm at non-compulsive frequency with intact sleep/function (consistent with ICD-11 thresholds) |
| Late-night gooning → low T | **TRUE, indirect** | Mechanism = stolen slow-wave sleep → less GH pulse + lower T (JAMA 2011 + Van Cauter 1996), not the act itself |

## Key citations

1. Leproult R, Van Cauter E. *Effect of 1 Week of Sleep Restriction on Testosterone Levels in Young Healthy Men.* JAMA. 2011;305(21):2173–2174. doi:10.1001/jama.2011.710 — 5h/night × 1 week → T −10–15%
2. Van Cauter E, Plat L. *Physiology of growth hormone secretion during sleep.* J Pediatr. 1996;128(5 Pt 2):S32-7. PMID 8627466 — ~70% GH pulses in SWS
3. Jiang M. *Periodic changes in serum testosterone levels after ejaculation in men.* Sheng Li Xue Bao. 2002;54(6):535-8. PMID 12506329 — n=28, day-7 peak 145.7% of baseline, temporary (source of viral "45%" claim)
4. Healthygamer/basic andrology 2021: *Hormonal response after masturbation in young healthy men – a randomized controlled cross-over pilot study.* Basic Clin Andrology. 2021. doi:10.1186/s12610-021-00148-2 — n=11, free-T slight effect, no ratio change
5. Voon V, et al. *Neural correlates of sexual cue reactivity in individuals with and without compulsive sexual behaviours.* PLOS ONE. 2014;9(7):e102419 — fMRI, wanting/liking dissociation, caveats apply
6. Kraus SW, et al. *Compulsive sexual behaviour disorder in the ICD-11.* World Psychiatry. 2018;17(1):109-110. doi:10.1002/wps.20499 — impulse-control dx; moral distress alone ≠ disorder; adolescent common masturbation ≠ disorder
7. Zacharopoulos Z, et al. *Pornography Consumption and Male Sexual Dysfunction: A Systematic Review.* Adv Exp Med Biol. 2026:1487:297-304. PMID 41273571 — frequency not predictor; problematic use matters more
8. Jacobs T, et al. *Associations Between Online Pornography Consumption and Sexual Dysfunction in Young Men.* JMIR Public Health Surveill. 2021;7(10):e32542 — n=3419, PPC→ED association; masturbation freq NS
9. Cleveland Clinic — *What Can (and Can't) Cause Low Testosterone* — masturbation does not lower T long-term
10. Kelly D (expert view, The Conversation, 2026) — nofap T-boost evidence = 2 weak studies, abstinence T effects not established

## Practical synthesis (for a 14-year-old on a looksmax protocol)

- **Harmful:** daily multi-hour edging after 9:30pm → cuts slow-wave sleep → lower GH/T next day + steals gym/homework hours + dopamine tolerance loop (Voon-style wanting/liking split is real only in *compulsive* users)
- **Neutral:** occasional (e.g. 1×/week), short, before 9:30pm, no compulsion, no functional impairment → below ICD-11 CSBD threshold, no measured T damage
- **Not a lever:** abstinence does not measurably raise baseline T; "nofap gains" = placebo/energy reallocation mostly
- **Real T/GH levers at 14:** sleep timing (protect 10pm–5:30 SWS window), lifting, calorie+protein surplus, low stress
