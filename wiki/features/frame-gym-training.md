# Frame Gym Training

## Lateral Deltoids
- Shoulders recover fast and can handle more frequency
- 3-4 days per week
- 3x12 lateral raises
- 3 second eccentric hold at top, 0.5 seconds
- Train till absolute failure with partials
- Cable or dumbbell, find what you like best

## Latissimus Dorsi
- 2-3 days per week
- 2x8 for both movements
- Lat pull overs and single arm lat pull down

### Lat Pullover
- Stretch the lats and move hips forwards as you pull over
- 3 second eccentric, 0.5 second hold

### Single Arm Lat Pull Down
- Crunch your ribs think "elbow to hip"
- 3 second eccentric, 0.5 second hold

## Rear Delts
- 3-4 days per week
- 2x12 for both movements

### Rear Delt Cable Fly
- Keep tension on rear delt
- 3 second eccentric, 0.5 second hold

### Face Pulls
- Eccentric 0.5 second hold
- Do this on cable

## Upper Chest
- 3x8 for both movements

### Reverse Grip Incline
- Focus on the stretch and explode out of the bottom

### Chest Flies
- Eccentric 0.5 second hold
- Do this on cable or if you have a good machine
