# Eye Whitening

## What We Ideally Want
- Clear, bright sclera with minimal redness or yellow discoloration
- A healthy white appearance rather than an unnaturally edited white
- Strong contrast between the sclera, iris, eyelashes and surrounding eye area
- Red or yellow sclera can make the eyes appear more tired, unhealthy and older
- White sclera was proven in studies to make you more attractive

## Improve Sclera Appearance Naturally

### Sleep
- Get consistent, adequate sleep
- Sleep deprivation can contribute to dry, irritated and red eyes

### Hydration
- Drink enough water throughout the day
- Proper hydration supports the tear film and reduces dryness

### Reduce Irritation
- Do not rub the eyes
- Limit cannabis use and vaping because they may worsen redness or dryness
- Reduce prolonged screen exposure
- Follow the 20 20 20 rule by looking 20 feet away for 20 seconds every 20 minutes
- Use lubricating artificial tears when the eyes feel dry

### Nutrition
- Maintain adequate omega 3 intake
- Maintain adequate vitamin A intake
- Maintain adequate vitamin C intake
- Correct deficiencies that may affect tear production

## Eye Drops and Quick Whitening

### Daily Maintenance
- Use lubricating artificial tears for dryness and irritation
- Choose products intended to moisturize the eye rather than aggressively constrict blood vessels
- Preservative free artificial tears may be preferable when drops are used frequently

### Special Occasions
- Brimonidine tartrate eye drops such as Lumify can temporarily reduce redness
- The whitening effect lasts for several hours
- Reserve redness relief drops for occasional use rather than relying on them every day
- Stop use and seek professional advice if they cause worsening redness, pain, visual changes or persistent irritation

> ⚠️ **Verified 2026-10-14 — the rebound risk is missing and it is significant.**
> Brimonidine works by vasoconstriction. Onset ~15 min, effect up to 10-12 hours.
>
> **The harm the wiki omits:** discontinuation or continued use can cause
> **rebound conjunctival congestion worse than baseline**, driving further use
> and creating a dependency cycle. This is why Lumify carries a **4-hour-per-eye
> maximum per day** on its package insert. Rare but real reports of
> **drug-induced glaucoma** exist (brimonidine lowers episcleral venous
> pressure).
>
> Occasional use for a specific occasion is reasonable. Daily use is not, and
> none of this works on genuinely yellow sclera — see below.

### Main Goal
- Fix the cause of redness first
- Maintain sleep, hydration and eye lubrication
- Use cosmetic redness relief only as an occasional finishing step
- Do not use drops to conceal persistent yellowing, eye pain or unexplained redness
