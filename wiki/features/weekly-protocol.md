## Quick rules

1. Azelaic Acid: Tue, Thu, Sat only (3×/week)
2. Oil Cleanser: Saturday only (1×/week)
3. Sunscreen: every morning, no exceptions
4. Apply everything to **damp skin** except azelaic acid (**dry skin**)
5. Hair serum: applied after shampoo → **Monday and Friday only**

## Every day (~25 min total)

- Eat 300 cal over TDEE + protein ~72g — eggs/chicken/milk/beans
- Hard gum 20–30 min — 10 min left + 10 min right minimum
- Tongue on palate, lips sealed, nose breathe (alarm 3×/day)
- Screens down 9:30pm → reddish lamp only → bed 10pm
- Wake 5:30 same time, nap 25 min after school
- Posture check ×3: stand tall, equal foot weight, head level

## 2× per week (FOOTBALL days: Sat, Sun — ~45 min)

- Football session (training or match)
- Neck curls 3×15–20 after football (or rest days)
- Progress: add 1 rep or tiny weight each week on neck curls

## Every week (Sunday, 5 min)

- Photo check: front (hair back, eye level, neutral) + side ×2 → compare monthly
- Weigh in: aim +0.5–1 lb/week
- Homework done before 9:30pm Fri–Sun too

## Every month

- Re-measure bigonial vs neck in photo — chewing/neck gains show ~8–12 weeks
- Adjust calories up if weight stalled

## Meal template (hit TDEE+300)

- Breakfast: eggs + bread/rice + milk
- Lunch: rice + meat/fish + veggies (biggest plate)
- Dinner: rice/noodles + protein (medium plate, done by 9pm)
- Snack: milk, banana, peanuts, yogurt
- 1 palm protein per meal first; chew evenly both sides, 20–30 bites; no gum during meals

## Focus at school (from Napoleon / workaholic video)

Rules taken from the 15 habits — only the ones that apply to a student, ranked by payoff:

1. **Complete concentration (habit 6)** — one subject until it's done, then the next. No phone, no second tab. Finish the problem set before opening anything else (strategy of the central position: all force on one point).
2. **Note-card flexibility (habit 14)** — before each week write: *if X happens → I do Y* (alarm fails → backup alarm; wifi dies → offline notes; test moves → which day I re-study). Stops the "everything broke, I quit" freeze.
3. **Compartmentalization (habit 1)** — one problem in your head at a time. Worries get written on paper *before* the block, then the drawer shuts. Worrying during class = double cost.
4. **Speed of starting (habit 8)** — start the assignment the day it's set, first 10 minutes ugly and fast. Procrastination, not slow work, eats the week.
5. **Systems over motivation (habit 7)** — same wake time, same first block, same warm-up routine. Don't renegotiate the plan daily.
6. **Ask for more (habit 9)** — ask the teacher for the past paper / extension / extra set. Worst answer: "no." Students lose by never asking.
7. **Study the greats (habit 15)** — copy a top student's actual system (notes, schedule, past papers), not their advice.
8. **Extreme rest (habit 13)** — real full-intensity work needs a real day off (Sunday). Rest as seriously as you work.
9. **Do NOT copy** — sleep 1–2h (habit 2) and skipping meals (habit 5). Sleep + food are what make the other 7 work.
