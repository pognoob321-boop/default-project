## HOLLOW CHEEKS: THE COMPLETE GUIDE

### What Creates Hollow Cheeks

Hollow cheeks = combination of:
1. **Low buccal fat** (deep cheek fat)
2. **Strong bone structure** (zygomatic bone + jawline)
3. **Tight buccinator and masseter muscles**
4. **Thin skin + low inflammation**

### The 4 Types of Hollow Cheeks (From looksmax.org)

#### Type 1: Outer Hollow Cheeks (Lighting-Based)
- "Model" V-contour
- Formed by shallow canine fossa + strong zygos
- Visible under indoor directional lighting
- Not truly sunken - hollows are light + bone structure
- Example: Fashion models, actors

#### Type 2: Inner Hollow Cheeks (Structural-Based)
- Found with flatter maxilla or sunken midface
- Truly sunken inward
- Visible under natural outdoor lighting
- Depth is structural, not lighting-based
- Example: Primal, fierce look

#### Type 3: Zygomatic Hollow (Bone-Driven)
- High cheekbone projection
- Light curves create shadows
- Requires good zygomatic mass

#### Type 4: Masseter-Driven Hollow
- Strong masseter muscles
- Creates "lines" in face
- Square/linear look vs angular/triangular
- Visible even without lighting

### The Ogee Curve
- The "S-curve" from cheekbone to jaw
- Created by protruding cheeks + chin contrasting jaw
- Key to hollow cheek aesthetic
- Requires good bone projection

## BUCCAL FAT REMOVAL (BICHECTOMY)

### What It Is
- Surgical removal of buccal fat pads
- Small, deep fat deposits in lower cheeks
- Between cheekbones and jawline
- Done via incisions inside mouth (no visible scars)

### Procedure Details
- Duration: 30-60 minutes
- Anesthesia: Local or general
- Cost: ~$3,000
- Recovery: 1-2 weeks
- Permanent: Fat pads don't regrow

### Ideal Candidates
- Adults 20-40
- Stable weight
- Full/round cheeks
- Good bone structure underneath
- NOT ideal if face already thin or over 50

### Community Consensus (From looksmax.org)

**Pros:**
- Can create hollow look if bone structure supports it
- 25-minute procedure
- Dramatic results for right candidates
- "Aged me in the best way" - one user

**Cons:**
- Ages poorly if over-removed
- Requires good bone structure to work
- Can look "sunken" or gaunt
- Not everyone gets hollow cheeks
- Subcutaneous fat vital for youthful skin

### The Controversy
```
Forum Debate:
"Buccal fat removal is a scam"
vs
"It worked for me because I had good bones"

Consensus:
- You NEED good bone structure first
- If you lack zygomatic projection, removal won't help
- Being lean first reveals if you can get hollow cheeks naturally
- Sub-10% bodyfat test: if no hollow cheeks, you need surgery
```

### Before Getting Bichectomy
1. Get to 10-12% bodyfat first
2. If still no hollow cheeks → consider buccal fat removal
3. If you have "baby face" (width peaks at cheeks, not zygos) → good candidate
4. If mandible is weak → buccal fat removal may cause "collapse"

### Alternatives to Bichectomy
| Method | Pros | Cons |
|--------|------|------|
| Cheek Lipo | Less invasive, salvageable | Less dramatic |
| Weight Loss | Free, natural | May not target buccal fat |
| Mewing | Free, safe | Limited effect |
| Implants | Add bone, not remove fat | More expensive |

## CHEEKBONE (ZYGOMATIC) IMPLANTS

### Types of Cheekbone Implants

#### 1. Submalar Implants
- Placed BELOW the malar eminence
- Gives "apple cheek" look
- More feminine appearance
- Most common off-the-shelf design
- **NOT recommended for males seeking hollow cheeks**

#### 2. Malar Implants
- Placed ON the zygomatic bone
- Creates angular, sharp appearance
- More masculine look
- Better for hollow cheek aesthetic
- Rare off-the-shelf, usually custom

#### 3. Malar Shell Implants
- Thin shell over zygomatic arch
- Most natural appearance
- Adds lateral projection
- Minimal anterior projection
- Best for "model" hollow cheek look

#### 4. Combined (Malar + Infraorbital)
- Covers cheekbone + under-eye area
- Provides full midface support
- Custom PEEK or Medpor
- Most comprehensive option

### Custom vs Off-the-Shelf

| Factor | Custom | Off-the-Shelf |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Fit | Perfect to your bone | Generic |
| Cost | $5,000-15,000+ | $2,000-5,000 |
| Natural look | Better | Often bloated |
| Surgeon skill needed | High | Moderate |
| Material | PEEK, Medpor, silicone | Various |

### Material Comparison

| Material | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| **PEEK** | Looks like bone, customizable | Expensive |
| **Medpor** | Porous, integrates with bone | Harder to remove |
| **Silicone** | Cheap, easy to remove | Can look fake, shifts |
| **Gore-Tex** | Soft, natural feel | Can absorb over time |

### Key Design Principles (From looksmax.org)
1. High-set lateral projection = natural look
2. Low-set anterior projection = bloated/feminine
3. Must extend along zygomatic arch
4. Transitions must be smooth (no "ball" look)
5. Must match palate width ratio

### Ideal Placement
```
GOOD: High on zygomatic arch, lateral projection
      → Creates shadow, hollow cheek effect
      → Natural angular appearance

BAD: Low on submalar, anterior projection
     → Apple cheek, bloated
     → Feminine appearance
```

## JAW IMPLANTS

### Types of Jaw Implants

#### 1. Jaw Angle (Gonial) Implants
- Placed at mandibular angle
- Creates wider, more angular jaw
- Can increase bigonial width
- Must match zygomatic width

#### 2. Chin Implants
- Adds projection to chin
- Can be vertical or horizontal
- Combined with jaw angles for harmony

#### 3. Wraparound Implants
- Covers chin + jaw angles
- Single piece design
- Can look unnatural if not custom
- May create "boxy" appearance

### The Ratio Rules

#### Bizygomatic-Bigonial Ratio
- Bizygomatic width should be ≥ bigonial width
- Ratio > 1 = uncanny (jaw wider than cheekbones)
- Ideal: Cheekbones slightly wider than jaw

> **Verified 2026-10-14.** The *direction* of this rule is correct — cheekbones
> should read wider than the jaw. The numbers used to justify it were not.
>
> - `facial-ratios.md` states bigonial = 85.5-92% of bizygomatic, then claims
>   this "implies" a bizygomatic:bigonial ratio of ~1.35. **That is an
>   arithmetic error.** 1/0.92 = 1.087, 1/0.855 = 1.170. The stated inputs imply
>   **1.09-1.17**, not 1.35.
> - Real CT morphometry lands at ~1.30: Sertel Meyvaci & Hizal 2021 (n=110,
>   males bizygomatic 137.6 mm / bigonial 106.0 mm = 1.30); Singh et al. 2025
>   (138.6 / 106.8 mm = 1.30).
>
> Both the 85.5-92% range and the 1.35 ratio are looksmax.org-derived. Neither
> is a published clinical norm.

#### Jaw-Neck Proportion
- Wide jaw requires thick neck
- "Pencil neck + wide jaw = unnatural"
- Genetics code for both together

### Why Jaw Implants Look Bad (From looksmax.org)

1. **Narrow palate** + wide implants = uncanny
2. **Ratio mismatch** (bigonial > bizygomatic)
3. **Implant too low** = boxy, feminine
4. **Not enough midface** to balance
5. **Recessed base** + implants = obvious

### Ideal Jaw Implant Design
- Conservative width increase
- High gonial angle placement
- Must harmonize with cheekbones
- Custom PEEK preferred
- Smooth transitions to chin

## SURGICAL BONE MODIFICATION

### Osteotomies (Cutting Bone)

#### 1. Le Fort I Osteotomy (Maxilla)
- Cuts upper jaw above teeth
- Allows forward/upward repositioning
- Improves midface projection
- Can correct bite + aesthetics
- Combined with BSSO for bimax

#### 2. BSSO (Bilateral Sagittal Split Osteotomy)
- Cuts lower jaw bilaterally
- Repositions mandible forward
- Creates sharp jawline
- Improves chin projection
- Most common jaw surgery

#### 3. Bimaxillary Advancement (Bimax)
- Le Fort I + BSSO combined
- Repositions both jaws
- Total transformation
- Expensive ($20,000-50,000+)
- 3-4 month recovery

#### 4. Genioplasty
- Slides chin bone forward/down
- Improves chin projection
- Can be vertical or horizontal
- More stable than chin implants

#### 5. Zygomatic Osteotomy (ZSO)
- Cuts cheekbone
- Repositions laterally
- More natural than implants
- Limited by existing bone thickness

### CCW Rotation (Counter-Clockwise)
- Rotates jaws upward and forward
- Shortens midface
- Projects chin dramatically
- Best for "long face" syndrome
- Gold standard for recessed cases

### MSE (Maxillary Skeletal Expander)
- Expands palate non-surgically
- Widens upper jaw
- Improves breathing
- Best during puberty
- Less effective in adults

## NON-SURGICAL METHODS

### Mewing (Tongue Posture)
- Free, safe, low effort
- Improves breathing + resting posture
- Minimal bone change in adults
- Worth doing as baseline

### Masseter Training
- Chewing hard foods/gum
- Can add 300x bone apposition risk *(CLAIM REMOVED 2026-10-14 — see note)*
- Increases ramus length during puberty
- Adds jaw width at any age
- Risk: antegonial notch

> **The "300x" figure has no source and has been deleted.** Checked against
> every bruxism/bone-apposition paper that could plausibly be meant:
> - **Turp 2021** (PMID 34663284): ~50% of mandibular angles showed apposition
>   in adult bruxism patients — but the control group was **adolescents aged
>   12-18**, not adult non-bruxers, so no bruxism-vs-nonbruxism odds ratio can
>   be computed from it.
> - **Hayek 2022** (PMID 36199841): 52% apposition — **no control group at all.**
> - **Popescu 2025** (PMID 40150044): ~3x bone apposition, 15x masseter
>   hypertrophy.
>
> 300x appears in none of them.
>
> **What the evidence does support:** masseter *muscle* hypertrophy is real.
> Adding *bone* width by chewing is not demonstrated in adults. The claim that
> it "adds jaw width at any age" is not supported.

### Body Fat Manipulation
- Sub-15% body fat reveals bone structure
- Sub-12% = maximum definition
- Most "mewing results" = fat loss

> **On the specific percentages (verified 2026-10-14):** lowering body fat does
> reveal facial structure — the *direction* is real and supported
> (Windhager 2013; de Jager 2018 meta-analysis, r=0.71 for perceived facial
> adiposity vs BMI). But **no peer-reviewed source sets a body-fat threshold for
> facial definition in men.** The literature uses BMI or facial morphometrics.
> The specific 10-12% / 12-15% / sub-10% numbers on this page are gym folklore
> that acquired decimal-point precision through repetition.

### Debloating
- ~~Reduce sodium intake~~ → supported (see below)
- Sleep on your side, not flat on your back
- Cut alcohol the night before
- ~~Increase water intake~~ → normal hydration only; water loading/cutting is counterproductive
- ~~Anti-inflammatory diet~~ → untested for facial puffiness
- Cold compresses
- Can reveal hidden bone structure

> **Verified 2026-10-14.** Only two levers have real measured evidence behind
> them: **sleep position** (postural fluid redistribution is the primary
> mechanism of morning facial puffiness) and **sodium**. Everything else on this
> list is untested. Full breakdown including deleted items:
> [Lip Improvement → Debloating](lip-improvement.md#debloating).

### Posture Correction
- Chin tucks
- Neck alignment
- Shoulder retraction
- Can improve jaw appearance immediately

## EVIDENCE-BASED PRIORITY STACK

### For Hollow Cheeks
1. **Get to 10-12% body fat** (free, reveals bones)
2. **Debloat** (free, reduces puffiness)
3. **Mewing** (free, improves posture)
4. **Masseter training** (free, adds width)
5. **If still no hollow cheeks** → Consider buccal fat removal + implants

### For Better Jawline
1. **Get lean** (reveals existing structure)
2. **Masseter training** (adds width)
3. **Posture correction** (improves appearance)
4. **If recessed** → BSSO/Bimax consultation
5. **If width needed** → Custom jaw angle implants

### For Better Cheekbones
1. **Get lean** (reveals existing structure)
2. **Debloat** (reduces puffiness)
3. **If flat** → Custom malar implants
4. **If minor deficit** → HA fillers (temporary)
5. **If severe** → Le Fort I or ZSO

## RISKS & CONTRAINDICATIONS

### Buccal Fat Removal Risks
- Over-removal = gaunt/aged look
- Asymmetry
- Nerve damage (rare)
- Cannot be reversed
- Ages poorly without bone support

### Implant Risks
- Infection
- Migration
- Asymmetry
- Unnatural appearance
- May need revision

### Jaw Surgery Risks
- Nerve damage (numbness)
- Infection
- Relapse
- Bite changes
- Long recovery (3-6 months)

### Contraindications
- Active acne/skin infection
- Bleeding disorders
- Unrealistic expectations
- Poor bone structure without planning
- Body dysmorphic disorder
