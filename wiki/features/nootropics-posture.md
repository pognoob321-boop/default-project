## Posture

### Thoracic / Shoulder Posture
- Upper back, ribs, and shoulders should stay aligned
- Shoulders stack over ribs
- Ribs stack over pelvis
- Upper back should not be overly rounded
- Chest should not cave in
- Shoulder blades should sit flat and slightly back/down

### Benefits:
- Makes frame look wider
- Makes neck look cleaner
- Opens the chest
- Makes upper body look more dominant
- Looks more confident and polished
- Improves breathing mechanics

### Forward Head Posture
- Head, neck, and shoulders should stay aligned
- Ear should stack over shoulder
- Chin should not jut forward
- Head should not sit in front of the shoulders
- Neck should stay tall, not compressed
- Upper traps should not tug upward
- Jaw should stay relaxed, not forced forward

### Benefits:
- Makes neck look longer and cleaner
- Improves jawline appearance
- Supports CCW rotation
- Makes posture look more confident
- Helps shoulders sit better
- Can reduce neck tightness and tension

### Pelvic / Full-Body Posture
- Ribs, pelvis, knees, and ankles should stay aligned
- Ribs should stack over pelvis
- Pelvis should stack over knees
- Knees should stack over ankles
- Ribs should not flare forward
- Hips should not dump into anterior pelvic tilt
- Lower back should not be overly arched
- Glutes and core should stay lightly engaged

### Benefits:
- Makes posture look taller and more balanced
- Reduces excessive lower-back arch
- Makes waist and hips look cleaner
- Improves visual proportions
- Helps shoulders and head sit better
- Makes movement look more athletic

## Mobility/Posture Work
- Stand with back against a wall
- Keep heels, glutes, upper back, and head close to the wall
- Tuck chin slightly so ears stack over shoulders
- Pull shoulders slightly back/down so they stack over ribs
- Exhale and bring ribs down so ribs stack over pelvis
- Lightly squeeze glutes/core so hips do not dump forward
- Hold 30-60 seconds, repeat 3-5 rounds daily

### Goal
- Ears over shoulders, shoulders over ribs, ribs over pelvis, pelvis over ankles

### Additional Exercises
- Thoracic extensions over foam roller: Improves upper-back mobility
- Hip flexor couch stretch: Opens tight hip flexors
