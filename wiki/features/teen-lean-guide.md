# How to Get Lean as a Teenager

## The Truth
At 14, your body is still growing. Focus on getting lean and athletic, not dangerously low body fat.
**Target range**: 12-17% body fat (healthy, athletic range for teens)

## 1. Training (4-5 Days/Week)

### Strength Training (3 Days) — Build Muscle First
Muscle burns more calories at rest than fat. More muscle = leaner look.

**Full Body Routine A (Mon/Thu):**
- Squats: 3x12
- Push-ups: 3x15
- Lunges: 3x10 each leg
- Plank: 3x45 sec
- Glute bridges: 3x15

**Full Body Routine B (Tue/Fri):**
- Pull-ups or rows: 3x8-10
- Dumbbell shoulder press: 3x12
- Bulgarian split squats: 3x10 each leg
- Side raises: 3x12
- Calf raises: 3x20

**Progressive overload**: Add 1-2 reps or a little weight each week.

### Cardio (2-3 Days) — Burn Extra Calories
- 20-30 min jog, bike, or jump rope
- Sprint intervals: 30 sec sprint / 60 sec walk x 8 rounds
- Or play sports (basketball, soccer, swimming)

## 2. Diet (The Real Game-Changer)

### Calories
- Don't crash diet — you're still growing
- Eat at maintenance or slight deficit (200-300 cal below maintenance)
- If you're active and eating clean, fat will drop naturally

### Protein (0.7-1g per lb of bodyweight)
- Eggs, chicken, beef, fish, Greek yogurt, beans
- Protein keeps you full and builds muscle
- Spread across 3-4 meals

### Carbs (Your Fuel)
- Rice, oats, potatoes, pasta, fruit
- Eat more around workouts
- Don't fear carbs — they power your training

### Fats (Keep Moderate)
- Olive oil, avocado, nuts, peanut butter
- Essential for hormones (testosterone)

### What to Cut/Reduce
- Soda, juice, energy drinks (liquid calories are the worst)
- Chips, candy, fast food
- Sugary cereals and snacks
- Late-night eating

### Example Day
- Breakfast: Eggs + oatmeal + fruit
- Lunch: Chicken + rice + vegetables
- Snack: Greek yogurt + nuts
- Dinner: Beef + potatoes + salad
- Before bed: Casein protein or cottage cheese

## 3. Sleep (Non-Negotiable)
- 8-10 hours per night
- Deep sleep = growth hormone + testosterone release
- Poor sleep = more belly fat, less muscle, more cravings
- Consistent bedtime every night

## 4. Lifestyle
- Walk 8,000-10,000 steps daily — easy calorie burn
- Drink water — 2-3 liters/day
- Get sunlight 10-20 min daily (vitamin D, hormones)
- Manage stress — cortisol causes fat storage

## 5. Supplements (Optional, Not Required)
- Creatine: 3-5g/day (strength, muscle, recovery)
- Vitamin D: 1000-2000 IU/day (hormones, bone health)
- Zinc: 10-15mg/day (testosterone, growth)
- Omega-3: 1-2g/day (inflammation, brain)
- Magnesium: 100-200mg before bed (sleep, recovery)

## What NOT to Do
- Don't starve yourself — you'll lose muscle and stunt growth
- Don't do extreme cardio — 2+ hours/day is counterproductive
- Don't skip meals — your body needs fuel to grow
- Don't take fat burners — dangerous for teens
- Don't compare to adults — you're still developing

## The Simple Formula
Get lean = Build muscle + Eat enough protein + Stay active + Sleep well + Be consistent

**Timeline**: 8-12 weeks to see noticeable changes. Results compound over months.
