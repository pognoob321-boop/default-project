# Facial Structure & Archetypes

## What is CCW Rotation?

Counterclockwise (CCW) rotation refers to the forward and upward growth of the maxilla (upper jaw) and the entire face.

Instead of the face growing downward and backward (clockwise), the midface rotates up and forward.

This improves midface fullness, chin projection, jawline definition, and overall facial balance.

## Why CCW Rotation is Desirable
- Shortens the appearance of the midface
- Improves under-eye support and cheek fullness
- Enhances chin projection and jawline sharpness
- Creates a more compact, youthful facial appearance
- Improves harmony between the upper, mid, and lower face

## Key Structural Changes with CCW Rotation
- Maxilla rotates upward and forward
- Mandible (lower jaw) rotates upward and forward
- Reduces lower facial height
- Improves the nasolabial angle
- Enhances airway space
- Creates a stronger, more defined jawline

## How to Achieve CCW Rotation

### 1. Foundation Habits
- Keep the full tongue resting on the palate, especially during sleep
- Breathe through the nose and keep lips sealed at rest
- Maintain light tooth contact or a relaxed lip seal
- Improve head, neck, and upper-back posture
- Be consistent daily, since posture habits matter over time

### 2. Reduce Clockwise Drivers
- Fix chronic mouth breathing if possible
- Address allergies, congestion, or airway issues
- Avoid low tongue posture and open-mouth posture
- Improve forward-head and slouched posture

### 3. Orthodontic Options (Growing Patients)
- Rapid palatal expansion when a narrow maxilla is present
- Facemask or maxillary protraction in aggregate younger patients
- Functional appliances if the bite pattern calls for them
- Early ENT / airway evaluation if airway restriction contributes to the pattern

### 4. Orthodontic Options (Older Teens & Adults
- MARPE or MSE expansion when indicated
- TAD-assisted molar intrusion / vertical control
- Combined orthodontics with bite correction
- Severe skeletal cases may require orthognathic surgery

## Facial Archetypes

### Cute
- Very low facial masculinity/dimorphism
- Soft jaw, softer bone structure, less angularity
- Big/innocent-looking eyes or youthful eye area
- Clear skin, good hair, no beard
- Frail or slim body rather than muscular

### Prettyboy
- Youthful, attractive face with some masculine structure
- Clear skin, good hair, polished/groomed appearance
- Shoulders narrower than hips, not overtly rugged
- Slightly deeper-set or more striking eyes
- Lean/slender body rather than frail or bulky

### Maesthetic
- High facial masculinity/dimorphism
- Broad jaw with strong facial structure
- Pronounced jawline and prominent lower third
- Deep-set or intense eyes
- Lean muscular or brawny physique

### Dom
- Very high facial masculinity/dimorphism
- Strong jaw, heavy brow, and rougher facial structure
- Muscular or visibly powerful body
- Tough demeanor and commanding presence
- Mature, dominant, and intimidating appearance

### Robust Prettyboy
- Basically just slightly higher dimo than prettyboy

### Ogre
- Extreme facial masculinity/dimorphism
- Very bulky or massive physique
- Thick beard or very short/bald hair
- Heavy brow, wide face, rougher features
- Physically imposing or high social dominance
