# Lip Improvement

## What We Ideally Want
- Full, symmetrical lips with defined borders
- Well-defined Cupid's bow
- Appropriate philtrum length (2/3 of upper lip or less)

## Without Permanent Surgery

### 1. Improve Hydration and Surface Appearance
- Keep lips hydrated with hyaluronic acid, peptides, or ceramides
- Use a gentle lip scrub 1-2x/week
- Use SPF lip balm during the day
- Avoid licking lips

### 2. Increase Apparent Fullness
- Gently massage lips to increase blood flow
- Use a lip plumper with mild irritants for temporary swelling
- Overline slightly with nude lip liner
- Avoid matte formulas; go for satin or gloss finishes

### 3. Expose More of the Upper Lip
- Practice pursing and stretching the upper lip
- Goal: make more of the pink lip show naturally

## With Procedures

### 1. Shorten a Long Philtrum
- Subnasal or bullhorn lip lift removes skin beneath the nose

### 2. Subtle Upper-Lip Lift
- Italian lip lift uses smaller incisions along nostril margins

### 3. Increase Vermilion Exposure
- Vermilion advancement removes skin above the lip border

### 4. Add Lip Projection
- V-Y advancement moves tissue forward from inside the mouth

### 5. Raise Downturned Mouth Corners
- Corner lip lift raises the oral commissures

### 6. Improve Upper-Lip Support
- Lip suspension procedures lift the upper lip using internal support

### 7. Permanently Increase Mouth Width
- Surgical mouth widening extends the oral commissures outward

## Daily Lip Softmax Routine

### Morning
- Apply hyaluronic acid serum to slightly damp lips
- Seal with Vaseline or fragrance-free lip balm
- Use SPF 30+ lip balm before going outside

### During the Day
- Reapply SPF lip balm after eating/drinking
- Stay hydrated

### Night
- Apply hyaluronic acid serum to damp lips
- Apply thick layer of Vaseline or overnight lip mask

### 2-3 Times Per Week
- Gently brush lips with soft toothbrush for 10-20 seconds
- Apply Vaseline immediately afterward

## Debloating

> **Superseded 2026-10-14 by a full evidence review:** [Facial Debloat Evidence
> Review](../research/facial-debloat-evidence.md). Read that one first — it
> corrects three claims in this section, including that **sleep duration does not
> cause puffiness** and that **carbohydrate/glycogen water is not a facial
> mechanism.** The dominant cause is gravity and sleep position, not diet.
>
> Short version that survives: **don't lie flat · walk in the evening · eat salt
> earlier rather than less · reduce alcohol · cold water in the morning.**

### What causes morning facial puffiness

Ranked by actual measured evidence:

| Rank | Cause | Evidence |
|------|-------|----------|
| **1** | **Gravity / lying supine** | **Dominant.** 3D scanning shows medial face loses 3.0 cc, lateral face gains 5.86 cc when supine. **80% of the shift completes within 2 h of lying down** — before you wake. Men are affected more than women. |
| **2** | Alcohol | ~5.5× skin blood flow increase (Gillespie 1967). Moderate drinking independently associated with under-eye puffiness in 3,267 women. |
| **3** | Sodium | Real but **weaker than folklore claims** — see note below. Amplifier, not primary cause. |
| **4** | Menstrual cycle | ~0.1–0.7 kg, located in trunk and arms. **Not the face.** |
| **5** | Glycogen water | Intracellular, muscle-specific. **Extracellular water does not change.** |

**Corrected — sleep duration does NOT cause puffiness.** Holding et al. 2019
(*J Sleep Res*, PMID 31006920), n=181 randomized: null on every facial variable.
Sleep deprivation makes skin **less hydrated and less red** — drier, not
swollen. You look tired, not puffy. Sleep for skin health, not for water.

**Corrected — sodium is weaker than this page previously claimed.** Heer et al.
2000 (*Am J Physiol Renal Physiol* 278:F585-595, **PMID 10751219**): 32 men,
randomized to 50–550 mEq NaCl/day. Plasma volume rose (+315 mL at top dose) but
**total body water and body mass did not increase at all** — the effect is a
shift from interstitial *into* intravascular space, i.e. potentially away from
the face. Salt matters for blood pressure and amplifies puffiness in higher-BMI
or sodium-depleted people, but "salt = puffy face" oversells it.

> **Note:** the widely-circulated PMID 10733739 sometimes attached to this paper
> is a different, unrelated study on malaria immunology. The correct PMID is
> 10751219.

### What actually works

1. **Don't lie flat — sleep on your side.** Follows directly from the dominant
   mechanism. No dedicated RCT, but the underlying fluid-shift data is the
   strongest in the field.
2. **Walk in the evening — the strongest lifestyle lever.** Redolfi et al. 2015
   (*AJRCCM* 191:856-858, PMID 25653112), randomized crossover: 1 week of
   **2 × 45 min daily walking** reduced overnight rostral fluid shift by
   **40%**, neck enlargement by **64%**, apnea severity by 30% — with **no
   weight change.** It's the calf muscle pump, not calories.
3. **Elevate your head.** 1 week of 18-inch head-of-bed elevation moved fluid
   caudally into the ankles (PMID 18953629).
4. **Cold water on the face** on waking. Vasoconstriction, temporary.
5. **Reduce alcohol.**

### Asian household — the main sodium vector

**Fish sauce (ម្លៃ)** is in nearly every dish. Halve it, or use lime plus a
pinch of salt.

Highest sodium density, in order: fish sauce → instant noodle packets (1,500–
2,000 mg) → preserved/salted fish, salted egg, Chinese sausage (ស៊ីកូ), dried
shrimp → pickled/fermented vegetables (ទឹកត្រាល់) → bouillon cubes, oyster
sauce, shrimp paste (kapi).

Flavour without salt: lime (កើម), tamarind, galangal, lemongrass, garlic,
chili, ginger, roasted peanuts.

**Best default dinner:** kuy teav (ក្អកឲ្យតេវ) — dry version, generous
meatball + meat + greens, sauce on the side.

**Eat salt earlier, don't cut it.** Bohmke et al. 2025 (*J Appl Physiol*):
a high-sodium meal eaten in the **morning** produced daytime fractional sodium
excretion of **0.84%** vs **0.35%** after the same meal eaten in the
**evening** (P=0.008). The kidney does not respond properly to an evening meal —
the authors call it "prolonged sodium retention." This is the best mechanistic
support for eating earlier.

### No evidence — do not bother

- Dandelion — pilot increased urination *frequency* but **not volume**; proper
  RCT null (P=0.13).
- Hibiscus — 6 RCTs, n=339, no clinical benefit (PMID 38878905).
- Bromelain, magnesium, potassium, vitamin B6 — no trials for facial puffiness.
- Cortisol/blame stress — no study exists. The real phenomenon is Cushing's.

### Deleted — actively counterproductive

- ~~Water loading then abruptly cutting water~~ — bloating **peaked during
  loading** (Reale 2018, PMID 29182412); extra mass loss only 0.6%.
- ~~Sodium loading then restriction~~ — combat-sport weight-cut, causes the
  rebound retention it is meant to prevent.
- ~~Reducing food volume and fiber~~ — gut content, not face fluid.
- ~~Carb depletion then loading~~ — the water is intracellular muscle water.

### When it is not a lifestyle problem

**The pitting test** — press a fingertip firmly into the swollen area for 5
seconds.

- **Pits** → sodium, heart failure, kidney disease, liver disease,
  **NSAIDs (ibuprofen/aspirin)**, calcium-channel blockers
- **Does not pit (boggy)** → **hypothyroidism**, lymphedema, angioedema

**NSAIDs are a common, under-discussed cause** of water retention. If you take
ibuprofen for anything, that is a likely contributor.

**Sudden lip or tongue swelling with difficulty breathing or swallowing = same-day
emergency.** ACE inhibitors (lisinopril, ramipril) can cause facial swelling with
no rash and no itching, hours to days after starting — and antihistamines and
steroids do **not** work for it.
