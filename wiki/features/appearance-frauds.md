# Appearance Frauds (Quick Upgrades)

## 1. Face Frauds That Actually Work

### Tinted Sunscreen
- Evens skin tone instantly
- Reduces redness, acne marks, and dullness
- Gives skin a cleaner "filtered" look without looking like makeup
- Best for guys with redness, hyperpigmentation, or uneven skin

### Eyebrow Pen
- Fills sparse areas
- Makes brows look denser and more masculine
- Improves eye area framing
- Use light strokes only, never draw a blocky brow

### Lumify
- Makes the whites of your eyes look brighter
- Reduces eye redness fast
- Makes the eyes look healthier and more awake
- Best before filming, photos, dates, or going out
- Don't overuse it daily

### Teeth Whitening
- Makes the lower face and smile look cleaner
- Yellow teeth lower your whole appearance
- White teeth create better contrast with lips and skin
- Use whitening strips or trays consistently

## 2. Height Frauds That Actually Work

### Elevator Boots
- Can add around 2.5 to 3.5 inches instantly
- Built-in lift is hidden inside the boot
- Best choice if you want the biggest realistic height boost
- Pair them with straight-leg or slightly stacked pants
- Choose chunky but believable boots, not obvious platforms

### Height Insoles
- Can add around 1 to 2 inches inside roomy shoes or boots
- Great for extra lift without changing your whole outfit
- Works best in high-top shoes or boots with extra room
- Too much lift in low-top shoes can look awkward and feel unstable

### How to Hit 5 Inches
- A 2-inch elevator boot plus a 2-inch insole gets you to 4-5 inches total
- Only stack lifts in supportive, roomy boots with enough internal space
- Longer pants help hide the added height naturally
- Comfort and balance matter more

## 3. Frame Frauds That Actually Work

### Structured Top Layers
- Structured jackets/overshirts
- Shoulder-padding/boxy shoulder cuts
- Layering to add upper-body width

### Better Shirt Choices
- Tapered waist clothing
- Dark fitted shirts
- Wider collars/crewnecks

### Gym + Photo Boosts
- Train side delts/lats/neck/traps
- Pump before photos

### What to Avoid + Balance
- Avoid skinny tight sleeves if arms are small
- Pants that balance shoulders and legs
