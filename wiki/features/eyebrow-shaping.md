# Eyebrow Shaping

## Eyebrow Tilt

### Negative Tilt
- Innocent, naive, unassertive look

### Positive Tilt
- Assertive, intense, mysterious look
- Goal: slightly positive tilt, around 5-13 degrees or flat
- Grow: upper outer-tail hairs
- Shave/pluck: lowest drooping tail hairs
- Effect: makes brows look less sad/tired and more lifted/intense

## Inner Brow Gap
- Goal: close enough to frame nose bridge, not unibrow
- Grow: inner brow heads if gap is too wide
- Shave/pluck: only clean center hairs that connect the brows
- Effect: reduces soft/feminine wide-gap look and makes gaze more intense

## Outer Brow Length
- Minimum vs ideal: brows extend past outer eye corners
- Grow: outer tail fullness
- Shave/pluck: do not cut the tail short; only remove messy low strays
- Effect: makes eye area look wider and better framed

## Low-Set Brow Look
- Goal: brows closer to eyes, around 0-0.66 eye heights above eye
- Grow: lower brow hairs, especially middle/lower edge
- Shave/pluck: avoid shaving underneath unless it is a random stray
- Effect: makes eye area look more compact and masculine

## Brow Thickness/Density
- Goal: thick, dense, natural brows
- Grow: leave brows untouched for 8-12 weeks
- Shave/pluck: remove only obvious strays, not structure hairs
- Effect: stronger eye framing, darker-looking brows, more masculine eye area
