# Height & Frame Frauds

## Height

### Elevator Shoes
- Use elevator shoes or naturally thick soled shoes for an instant height boost
- Adds 2-5 inches with no effort

### Discreet Heel Lifts
- Add discreet heel lifts or insoles inside shoes with enough space
- Comfortable, unnoticeable in most cases, and work with everyday footwear
- Prioritize comfort, stability, and natural proportions
- Avoid using so much height that walking looks stiff or unnatural

### Stacked Shoes
- Some boots and sneakers come with built-in extra height
- Practice walking before wearing lifts socially
- Maintain upright posture so the added height looks natural

## Shoulder Width

### Use subtle shoulder pads
- Use subtle shoulder pads to increase apparent width
- Keep padding mainly over the outer deltoids and make it symmetrical

### Wear structured garments
- Wear structured jackets, coats, and overshirts that hold a clean shoulder line
- Choose cuts with slight structure rather than completely soft, unstructured shapes

### Layer to add upper body bulkiness
- Layer clothing to add upper body bulkiness

### Avoid oversized clothing
- Avoid clothing that is far at or outside the natural shoulder line

## Waist and V Taper

### Use dark, fitted layers
- Use dark, fitted layers around the waist so the shoulders appear wider
- Choose tops that fit closer around the waist while staying comfortable

### Tuck or partially tuck shirts
- Tuck or partially tuck shirts when it improves proportions

### Use jackets that taper inward
- Use jackets that taper inward below the chest
- Avoid bulky tops and thick layers around the stomach

## Chest, Arms and Clothing Fit

### Use fitted sleeves to make the arms appear larger
- Wear shirts with sleeves that sit closer to the biceps without squeezing

### Choose thicker fabrics that hold shape around the chest
- Woven shirts and structured knits can emphasize the chest more than thin clingy fabrics

### Keep clothing proportions balanced on the frame
- Keep clothing proportions balanced on the frame so it looks natural

## Posture

### Keep the head stacked over the shoulders
- Keep the head stacked over the shoulders
- Keep the chest open without over-flaring the ribs
- Keep ribs stacked over pelvis

### Avoid rounded shoulders
- Avoid rounded shoulders because they reduce visible width
- Avoid a forward-head position that shortens the neck

### Practice standing and moving
- Practice standing and moving in the full outfit before going out

## Face and Grooming

### Quick appearance upgrades that improve presentation and contrast
- Use a hair style that suits face shape and density
- Use a facial cleanser to control oil and maintain clear skin
- Apply moisturizer every morning and night
- Keep facial hair neat or clean-shaven

### Eyebrows and Eyelashes
- Use brow grooming to keep eyebrows clean and defined
- Use lash curler to open the eye area and look more awake

### Eye Area
- Get enough sleep to reduce puffiness and dark circles
- Use caffeine serum or cooling patches for a temporary fix

### Skin
- Use sunscreen every morning to prevent uneven tone and early aging
- Use a mild chemical exfoliant 1-2 times a week to keep skin smooth

### Teeth and Smile
- Brush twice daily and floss once a day
- Use whitening toothpaste or strips for mild brightening

### Coloring
- Match clothing colors to hair, eyebrow, eye, skin tone
- Use contrast between clothing and skin to create more visual interest

## Status and Presentation Fraud

### Clothing
- Wear clean, fitted clothing that improves your proportions
- Use structured jackets, coats, and overshirts for a more dominant silhouette

### Accessories
- Use simple jewelry to make the outfit look more complete
- Match metals to keep the look cohesive

### Fragrance and Cleanliness
- Use fragrance before social events or dates
- Apply fragrance to clean skin for better longevity

### Voice and Speech
- Speak clearly and at an audible volume
- Use a calm and controlled speaking pace

### Body Language
- Stand upright with the head stacked over the shoulders
- Keep hands visible and gestures controlled

### Social Proof
- Be seen interacting comfortably with other people
- Share photos or stories that signal a fun and active social life
