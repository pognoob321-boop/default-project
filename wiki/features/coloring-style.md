# Coloring & Style Optimization

## Color Theory for Personal Coloring

### Contrast Levels
- Low Contrast: dark hair + dark skin
- Medium Contrast: medium tones
- High Contrast: dark hair + light skin + bright eyes

### Seasonal Color Analysis
- Winter: Dark hair + light skin + bright eyes
- Summer: Light hair + light skin + soft eyes
- Autumn: Red, auburn, or golden hair + warm skin
- Spring: Light golden hair + warm skin + bright eyes

### If Rocking Blonde Hair
- Having darker colored eyebrows is a necessity

## Tanning

### Peak UV Hours
- 10am - 4pm are usually peak UV hours
- Have the strongest rays, makes you age & increases cancer risk

### Diet for Skin Health
- Carotenoids (carrots, sweet potatoes)
- Vitamin C (Oranges, Citrus etc.)
- Omega-3's (Salmon, mackerel, walnuts)

## Fading a Tan
- Mild scrubs or light chemical scrubs (AHA/BHA) a few times a week
- Moisturize daily to keep your skin plump
- Vitamin C for collagen synthesis & damage healing
- Vitamin K helps with minor skin discoloration
- Retinoids/Carotenoids promotes cell repair
- Amino acids from protein-rich foods for skin repair (collagen & elastin)

## Facial Hair — ⚠️ CORRECTED 2026-10-17

**The old "by face shape" rules below had no citations and are not supported by research.**
No study has ever tested whether a given beard style suits a given face shape.

What the evidence actually shows ([Hair & Facial Hair Attractiveness Evidence](../research/hair-facial-hair-attractiveness.md)):

1. **Heavy stubble rates highest; clean-shaven rates lowest.** Dixson et al. 2016 (N = 8,520 women)
   and Boothroyd et al. 2013 found the same ordering: heavy stubble > light stubble > full beard >
   clean-shaven.
2. **The real reason to wear a beard is masking, not jawlines.** Facial hair dampens the
   attractiveness penalty for both unusually masculine *and* unusually feminine face shapes
   (facial hair × masculinity, p < .001). It helps whatever shape you have.
3. **More beard = more masculine, but not more attractive.** Masculinity ratings rise linearly with
   facial hair; attractiveness does not. Do not optimise the linearity.
4. **Conforming to local beard fashion costs you.** Beards were rated more attractive when beards
   were rare in the stimulus set (negative frequency-dependent preference).
5. **Full beard wins for long-term and fathering ratings**, if that is the goal.

### Retained aesthetic heuristics (unverified, kept for practicality)
- Long/tall face: shorter beard, tapered at the cheeks
- Round/wide face: more length below the chin, shorter on the sides
- Keep the neckline clean regardless of jawline
- These are style-plausibility rules, not attractiveness findings
