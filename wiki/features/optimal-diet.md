# Optimal Diet

## High Carb, High Protein, Lowish Fat
Fuel growth. Support hormones. Maximize potential.

## Calorie Surplus
- Eat more than maintenance
- Aim for +250 to +500 calories/day
- Adjust based on bodyweight progress
- Track and be consistent

## Top Protein Sources
High quality. Supports muscle, hormones, and recovery.
1. Chicken Breast
2. Lean Beef
3. Eggs

## Top Carb Sources
Main energy source. Fuels training, brain, and hormones.
1. White Rice
2. Oats
3. Potatoes

## Good Fat Sources (Keep Lowish)
Essential for hormone production and overall health. Keep in moderation.
1. Olive Oil
2. Avocado
3. Nuts

## Meal Timing (Not Crucial, But Helpful)
- Spread protein throughout the day
- Eat around training
- Stay consistent
- Example day (4-6 meals): Breakfast - Snack - Lunch - Pre-Workout - Post-Workout - Dinner - Before Bed
