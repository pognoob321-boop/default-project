# Hair Growth & Loss

## How Hair Loss and Hair Growth Actually Happens

### The Hair Growth Cycle
- Anagen (Growth Phase): 2-6+ years
- Catagen (Transition Phase): 2-3 weeks
- Telogen (Resting Phase): 2-3 months
- Hair follicles shrink over time

### Why Hair Loss Happens (AGA)
- DHT (dihydrotestosterone) binds to androgen receptors in genetically susceptible follicles
- This causes miniaturization - shrinks the follicle over time
- Results in shorter, thinner, weaker hair strands
- Eventually follicles become too small to produce visible hair

### The Role of DHT
- DHT is a byproduct of testosterone
- Made by 5-alpha-reductase enzymes
- 3-5x more potent than testosterone
- Binds to receptors in scalp follicles
- Triggers inflammation, chronically follicles, miniaturization

### How Hair Growth is Stimulated
- Increase blood flow to the follicle
- Prolong the anagen (growth) phase
- Improve nutrient delivery
- Reduce inflammation and oxidative stress
- Block DHT locally or systemically without killing activity

## Derma Stamping for Absorption + Hair Growth

### How a Derma Stamp Helps Absorption
- Creates tiny micro-channels in the skin
- Helps topical products reach the follicle more effectively
- May improve local blood flow and signaling
- Supports absorption, but is not instant regrowth

### Step by Step: Growing Eyebrows
1. Clean the area and sanitize the derma stamp
2. Use 0.25-0.5 mm for eyebrows
3. Stamp lightly in sparse areas, 1-2x weekly
4. Wait 12-24 hours before applying strong actives like finasteride
5. Use your eyebrow growth products consistently on non-stamping days

### Step by Step: Growing Scalp Hair
1. Start with a clean scalp and sanitized derma stamp
2. Use 0.5-1.0 mm depending on sensitivity
3. Stamp thinning areas evenly, 1x weekly
4. Let the scalp recover before applying topicals
5. Stay consistent with your main treatment plan
