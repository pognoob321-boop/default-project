# Nose Slimming & Under-Eye Bags

> Verified 2026-10-14. The hydrocortisone protocol previously on this page was
> removed — see [Removed: Hydrocortisone](#removed-hydrocortisone) below.

## Removed: Hydrocortisone

**The former "1. Hydrocortisone, 5-15 minutes, 1-3x/week" protocol has been
deleted. Do not do this.**

- **No evidence of benefit.** No RCT. Nasal appearance is bone and cartilage;
  5-15 minutes of topical steroid cannot alter either.
- **Real harm.** Chronic topical corticosteroid on thin nasal-dorsum skin causes
  cutaneous atrophy, telangiectasia, and steroid rosacea / perioral dermatitis.
  Intermittent "before events" facial steroid use is a textbook trigger for
  topical steroid-induced rosacea.

If you want to reduce *apparent* nose size, the only non-medical lever is
reducing facial water retention and body fat — see
[Lip Improvement](lip-improvement.md#debloating).

## Tretinoin / Retinoids
Long-term skin-quality aid for the nose.

### Main Idea
- Retinoids may make the nose look smaller in some cases depending on skin type
- The effect of retinoid regimen rather than a nose-only procedure

### How Tret Would Help
- Improves skin turnover: texture, clogged pores, oiliness, and rough skin
- The "smaller nose" effect would mostly come from smoother, tighter-looking skin
- Helps reduce a thick or oily-looking nose appearance
- Does not shrink cartilage or structurally change the nose

## Standard Under-Eye Discoloration Foundation

### Caffeine (Temporary Vascular Support)
- Best for vascular-based blueness under the eyes (often called dark circles)
- Reduces puffiness by vasoconstriction
- Temporarily shrinks dilated blood vessels

### Vitamin K + Caffeine (Vascular Support)
- Battle for vascular-based blueness under the eyes
- After 8 weeks, subjects had reduced dark circle intensity and depth

### Vitamin C (Brightening / Collagen Support)
- Helps抑制 excess pigment by brightening and supporting collagen
- Improves thickness and firmness of under-eye skin
- Shown to increase under-eye brightness

### Niacinamide (Pigment + Barrier Support)
- Helps reduce pigment transfer in the skin, helping with dark circles
- Also improves the barrier, something that helps with sensitivity when using retinoids for under-eye circles

### Glycolic Acid Peels (Stronger Pigment Option)
- Used in studies for under-eye pigmentation
- Studied concern: 20% glycolic acid peel with alpha C applied 2x weekly for 4 weeks (2002)
- Results: improvement in pigmented circles vs the gluconolactone group

## Standard Under-Eye Bag Foundation

### Caffeine (Temporary Puffiness Help)
- Best for temporary fat puffiness
- Can temporarily reduce puffiness and vascular pooling
- One of the better-supported topical ingredients for under-eye appearance

### Hyaluronic Acid Filler (Tear Trough Hollowing)
- Best for hollow tear-trough shadow
- Can improve under-eye shadows and tired look
- A randomized multicenter study found VYC-15L HA filler safe and effective
- Improved moderate/severe infraorbital hollowing through 1 year

### Lower Blepharoplasty / Fat Repositioning (Surgical Option)
- Best for real fat eye bags
- Strongest structural fix
- Restores under-eye skin and orbital fat regulation
- Commonly removes under-eye eye bags and circles

### Retinoid / NA / Peptides / Moisturizer (Supportive Option)
- Best for under-eye looseness from crepey skin
- Can make under-eye skin look smoother and more hydrated
- Improves skin texture and fine line-related bag appearance
- Does not remove true fat pad protrusion

## Simple Daily Under-Eye Routine (3 Steps Only)

### 1. Morning - Caffeine
- Reduces puffiness and vascular darkness
- Constricts blood vessels for a temporary improvement
- Apply a pea-sized amount and gently pat around the orbital bone

### 2. Morning - Vitamin C
- Brightens under-eye area and helps with dark circles
- Supports collagen and improves overall skin quality
- Gently pat around the under-eye area

### 3. Night - Retinal / Retinol
- Improves fine lines, texture, and thin skin
- Boosts collagen and strengthens over time
- Use 2-3x per week to start, then increase as tolerated
- Apply a pea-sized amount around the orbital bone
