## 0. Curiosity as Compass: The Video Thesis

### 0.1 The Stated Goal

> **Video (lines 187-191)**: \Research can actually feel rewarding as well. It should not feel like a chore. It should be almost like a game for your curiosity. And that is what I want you to learn by the end of this video.\

### 0.2 Genuine Interest vs. Obligation

- **Research needs curiosity. And I mean genuine interest.** (line 212)
- The creator admits: \In the past I would write down a list of things that I thought I should know and I went out to study them. And I forced myself. I did not even really want to do half of these... It was based on my idea of what was good not what I actually enjoyed.\
- **Ubisoft analogy (lines 224-226)**: \I treated learning like a Ubisoft game something that I had to 100% complete for some reason and that just completely killed my motivation.\
- **Listen to your curiosity** - the natural urge from within. The things you think about, care about, want to talk about. That should pull you naturally.
- It does not matter what is on your feed, what people laugh at online, what parents want. It must come from within. \It is like swimming with the current.\
